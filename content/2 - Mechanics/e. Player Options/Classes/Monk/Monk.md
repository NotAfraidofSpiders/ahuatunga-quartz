# Class Features

As a monk, you gain the following class features.

## Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per monk level\
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier\
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per monk level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** None\
**Weapons:** [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Simple weapons]], [[Weapons#Shortsword|shortswords]]\
**Tools:** Choose one type of [[Tools#Artisan’s Tools|artisan's tools]] or one [[Tools#Musical Instrument|musical instrument]]\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Strength, Dexterity]]\
**Skills:** Choose two from [[Ability Scores#Skills|Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, and Stealth]]

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a [[Weapons#Shortsword|shortsword]] or (b) any [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple weapon]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Dungeoneer's Pack|dungeoneer's pack]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Explorer's Pack|explorer's pack]]
- 10 [[Weapons#Dart|darts]]

## Unarmored Defense

Beginning at 1st level, while you are wearing no [[Armor Rules#Armor|armor]] and not wielding a [[Armor#Shield|shield]], your AC equals 10 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Dexterity modifier]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Wisdom modifier]]. ^56bcae

## Martial Arts

At 1st level, your practice of martial arts gives you mastery of combat styles that use unarmed strikes and monk weapons, which are [[Weapons#Shortsword|shortswords]] and any [[Weapons#Simple Melee Weapons|simple melee weapons]] that don't have the [[Weapon Rules#Two-Handed|two-handed]] or [[Weapon Rules#Heavy|heavy]] property. ^ce1dd1

You gain the following benefits while you are unarmed or wielding only monk weapons and you aren't wearing armor or wielding a shield: ^ebb78e

- You can use [[Ability Scores#Dexterity|Dexterity]] instead of [[Ability Scores#Strength|Strength]] for the [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] and [[Combat Rules#Damage Rolls|damage]] rolls of your [[Combat Rules#Unarmed strike|unarmed strikes]] and monk weapons.
  ^bc5b2d
- You can roll a d4 in place of the [[Combat Rules#Unarmed strike|normal damage of your unarmed strike]] or monk weapon. This die changes as you gain monk levels, as shown in the Martial Arts column of the [[Monk#Monk Progression Table|Monk table]].
  ^b07d4c
- When you use the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]] with an [[Combat Rules#Unarmed strike|unarmed strike]] or a monk weapon on your turn, you can make one unarmed strike as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]]. For example, if you take the Attack action and attack with a [[Weapons#Quarterstaff|quarterstaff]], you can also make an unarmed strike as a bonus action, assuming you haven't already taken a bonus action this turn. ^b5e37c

Certain monasteries use specialized forms of the monk weapons. For example, you might use a club that is two lengths of wood connected by a short chain (called a nunchaku) or a sickle with a shorter, straighter blade (called a kama). Whatever name you use for a monk weapon, you can use the game statistics provided for the weapon on the [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Weapons Table]]

## Ki

Starting at 2nd level, your training allows you to harness the mystic energy of ki. Your access to this energy is represented by a number of ki points. Your monk level determines the number of points you have, as shown in the Ki Points column of the [[Monk#Monk Progression Table|Monk table]].

#### Homebrew Revision (Optional):

- The number of ki points you have equals your monk level + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Wisdom modifier]] ^f49a1f

You can spend these points to fuel various ki features. You start knowing three such features: [[Monk#Flurry of Blows|Flurry of Blows]], [[Monk#Patient Defense|Patient Defense]], and [[Monk#Step of the Wind|Step of the Wind]]. You learn more ki features as you gain levels in this class. ^b1187c

When you spend a ki point, it is unavailable until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, at the end of which you draw all of your expended ki back into yourself. You must spend at least 30 minutes of the rest meditating to regain your ki points. ^bba8e6

Some of your ki features require your target to make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] to resist the feature's effects. The saving throw DC is calculated as follows: ^46a2c0

**Ki save DC** = 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers|Wisdom modifier]] ^51c215

### Flurry of Blows

Immediately after you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]] on your turn, you can spend 1 ki point to make two [[Monk#Martial Arts|unarmed strikes]] as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]]. ^9d4064

### Patient Defense

You can spend 1 ki point to take the [[Combat Rules#Dodge|Dodge action]] as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] on your turn. ^5013d0

### Step of the Wind

You can spend 1 ki point to take the [[Combat Rules#Disengage|Disengage]] or [[Combat Rules#Dash|Dash]] action as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] on your turn, and your [[Movement Rules#Jumping|jump distance]] is doubled for the turn.

## Unarmored Movement

Starting at 2nd level, your [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] increases by 10 feet while you are not wearing [[Armor Rules#Armor|armor]] or wielding a [[Armor#Shield|shield]]. This bonus increases when you reach certain monk levels, as shown in the [[Monk#Monk Progression Table|Monk table]]. ^eab227

At 9th level, you gain the ability to move along vertical surfaces and across liquids on your turn without [[Environment Rules#Falling|falling]] during the move. ^d557cf

## Dedicated Weapon (Optional)

Also at 2nd level, you train yourself to use a variety of weapons as monk weapons, not just [[Weapons#Simple Melee Weapons|simple melee weapons]] and [[Weapons#Shortsword|shortswords]]. Whenever you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, you can touch one [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|weapon]], focus your ki on it, and then count that weapon as a [[Monk#Martial Arts|monk weapon]] until you use this feature again. ^9199d0

The chosen weapon must meet these criteria: ^2aa506

- The [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|weapon]] must be a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple or martial weapon]]. ^b19b51
- You must be [[Weapon Rules#Weapon Proficiency|proficient]] with it. ^5f72f5
- It must lack the [[Weapon Rules#Heavy|heavy]] and [[Weapon Rules#Special|special]] properties. ^4e4a92

## Monastic Tradition

When you reach 3rd level, you commit yourself to a monastic tradition. Your tradition grants you features at 3rd level and again at 6th, 11th, and 17th level.

## Deflect Missiles

Starting at 3rd level, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to deflect or catch the missile when you are hit by a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged weapon attack]]. When you do so, the damage you take from the attack is reduced by 1d10 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Dexterity modifier]] + your monk level. ^eac3a5

If you reduce the damage to 0, you can catch the missile if it is small enough for you to hold in one hand and you have at least one hand free. If you catch a missile in this way, you can spend 1 [[Monk#Ki|ki point]] to make a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] with a [[Weapon Rules#Range|range]] of 20/60 using the weapon or piece of ammunition you just caught, as part of the same reaction. You make this attack with [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency]], regardless of your weapon proficiencies, and the missile counts as a [[Monk#Martial Arts|monk weapon]] for the attack. ^f6d581

## Ki-Fueled Attack (Optional)

Also at 3rd level, if you spend 1 ki point or more as part of your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] on your turn, you can make one attack with an [[Monk#Martial Arts|unarmed strike or a monk weapon]] as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] before the end of the turn. ^706209

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Slow Fall

Beginning at 4th level, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] when you fall to reduce any [[Environment Rules#Falling|falling damage]] you take by an amount equal to five times your monk level. ^222939

## Quickened Healing (Optional)

Also at 4th level, as an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can spend 2 [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] and roll a [[Monk#Martial Arts|Martial Arts die]]. You regain a number of hit points equal to the number rolled plus your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]]. ^0a22c9

## Extra Attack

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]] on your turn.

## Stunning Strike

Starting at 5th level, you can interfere with the flow of ki in an opponent's body. When you hit another creature with a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee weapon attack]], you can spend 1 ki point to attempt a stunning strike. The target must succeed on a Constitution [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] or be [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] until the end of your next turn.

## Focused Aim (Optional)

Also at 5th level, when you miss with an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], you can spend 1 to 3 [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] to increase your attack roll by 2 for each of these ki points you spend, potentially turning the miss into a hit.

## Ki-Empowered Strikes

Starting at 6th level, your [[Monk#Martial Arts|unarmed strikes]] count as magical for the purpose of overcoming [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Evasion

At 7th level, your instinctive agility lets you dodge out of the way of certain area effects, such as a blue dragon's lightning breath or a [[Spells#Fireball|fireball]] spell. When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.

## Stillness of Mind

Starting at 7th level, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to end one effect on yourself that is causing you to be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]].

## Purity of Body

At 10th level, your mastery of the ki flowing through you makes you immune to [[Diseases|disease]] and [[Poisons|poison]].

## Tongue of the Sun and Moon

Starting at 13th level, you learn to touch the ki of other minds so that you understand all spoken languages. Moreover, any creature that can understand a language can understand what you say.

## Diamond Soul

Beginning at 14th level, your mastery of ki grants you [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency]] in all [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]].

Additionally, whenever you make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] and fail, you can spend 1 [[Monk#Ki|ki point]] to reroll it and take the second result.

## Timeless Body

At 15th level, your ki sustains you so that you suffer none of the frailty of old age, and you can't be aged magically. You can still die of old age, however. In addition, you no longer need [[Environment Rules#Food and Water|food or water]].

## Empty Body

Beginning at 18th level, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to spend 4 [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] for 1 minute. During that time, you also have [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to all damage but force damage.

Additionally, you can spend 8 [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] to cast the _[[Spells#Astral Projection|astral projection]]_ spell, without needing [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|material components]]. When you do so, you can't take any other creatures with you.

## Perfect Self

At 20th level, when you roll for [[Combat Rules#Initiative|initiative]] and have no [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] remaining, you regain 4 ki points.
