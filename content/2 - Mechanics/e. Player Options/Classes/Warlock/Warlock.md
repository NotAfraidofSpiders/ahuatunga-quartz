**_Warlocks are seekers of the knowledge that lies hidden in the fabric of the multiverse. Through pacts made with mysterious beings of supernatural power, warlocks unlock magical effects both subtle and spectacular._**

_You must have a [[Ability Scores#Charisma|Charisma]] score of 13 or higher in order to [[Character Advancement Rules#Multiclassing|multiclass]] in or out of this class._

# Class Features

As a warlock, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per warlock level\
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier\
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per warlock level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** [[Armor Rules#Light Armor|Light armor]]\
**Weapons:** [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Simple weapons]]\
**Tools:** None\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Wisdom, Charisma]]\
**Skills:** Choose two from [[Ability Scores#Skills|Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, and Religion]]

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a [[Weapons#Light Crossbow|light crossbow]] and 20 [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|bolts]] or (b) any [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple weapon]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Component Pouch|component pouch]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Arcane Focus|arcane focus]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Scholar's Pack|scholar's pack]] or (b) a [[Adventuring Gear#Dungeoneer's Pack|dungeoneer's pack]]
- [[Armor#Leather Armor|Leather armor]], any [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple weapon]], and two [[Weapons#Dagger|daggers]]

## Pact Magic

Your arcane research and the magic bestowed on you by your patron have given you facility with spells.

### Cantrips

You know two cantrips of your choice from the [[Warlock Spell List|warlock spell list]]. You learn additional warlock [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrips]] of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the [[Warlock#Warlock Progression Table|Warlock table]].

### Spell Slots

The Warlock table shows how many [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slots]] you have to cast your [[Warlock#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|warlock spells]] of 1st through 5th level. The table also shows what [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|the level of those slots]] is; all of your spell slots are the same level. To cast one of your warlock spells of 1st level or higher, you must expend a spell slot. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest.

For example, when you are 5th level, you have two 3rd-level spell slots. To [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast]] the 1st-level spell _[[Spells#Witch Bolt|witch bolt]]_, you must spend one of those slots, and you cast it as a 3rd-level spell.

### Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher

At 1st level, you know two 1st-level spells of your choice from the [[Warlock Spell List|warlock spell list]].

The Spells Known column of the [[Warlock#Warlock Progression Table|Warlock table]] shows when you learn more warlock spells of your choice of 1st [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|level]] or higher. A spell you choose must be of a level no higher than what's shown in the table's Slot Level column for your level. When you reach 6th level, for example, you learn a new warlock spell, which can be 1st, 2nd, or 3rd level.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the warlock spells you know and replace it with another spell from the warlock spell list, which also must be of a level for which you have [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slots]].

### Spellcasting Ability

[[Ability Scores#Charisma|Charisma]] is your spellcasting ability for your [[Warlock#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|warlock spells]], so you use your Charisma whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Charisma [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers|modifier]] when setting the [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] DC for a warlock spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.

**Spell save DC** = 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Charisma modifier]]

**Spell attack modifier** = your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Charisma modifier]]

### Spellcasting Focus

You can use an [[Adventuring Gear#Arcane Focus|arcane focus]] as a spellcasting focus for your warlock spells.

## Eldritch Invocations

In your study of occult lore, you have unearthed [[Eldritch Invocations]], fragments of forbidden knowledge that imbue you with an abiding magical ability.

At 2nd level, you gain two eldritch invocations of your choice. When you gain certain warlock levels, you gain additional invocations of your choice, as shown in the Invocations Known column of the [[Warlock#Warlock Progression Table|Warlock table]]. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the invocations you know and replace it with another invocation that you could learn at that level.

## Pact Boon

At 3rd level, your otherworldly patron bestows a gift upon you for your loyal service. You gain one of the following features of your choice.

### Pact of the Blade

- You can use your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to create a pact weapon in your empty hand. You can choose the form that this [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|melee weapon]] takes each time you create it. You are [[Weapon Rules#Weapon Proficiency|proficient]] with it while you wield it. This weapon counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.
- Your pact weapon disappears if it is more than 5 feet away from you for 1 minute or more. It also disappears if you use this feature again, if you dismiss the weapon (no action required), or if you die.
- You can transform one magic weapon into your pact weapon by performing a special ritual while you hold the weapon. You perform the ritual over the course of 1 hour, which can be done during a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short rest]].
- You can then dismiss the weapon, shunting it into an extradimensional space, and it appears whenever you create your pact weapon thereafter. You can't affect an artifact or a sentient weapon in this way. The weapon ceases being your pact weapon if you die, if you perform the 1-hour ritual on a different weapon, or if you use a 1-hour ritual to break your bond to it. The weapon appears at your feet if it is in the extradimensional space when the bond breaks.

### Pact of the Chain

- You learn the _[[Spells#Find Familiar|find familiar]]_ spell and can cast it as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Rituals|ritual]]. The spell doesn't count against your number of [[Warlock#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|spells known]].
- When you cast the spell, you can choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: imp, pseudodragon, quasit, or sprite.
- Additionally, when you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]], you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack with its [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]].

### Pact of the Tome

- Your patron gives you a grimoire called a Book of Shadows. When you gain this feature, choose three [[Spells#Cantrips|cantrips]] from any class's spell list (the three needn't be from the same list). While the book is on your person, you can cast those cantrips at will. They don't count against your number of [[Warlock#Cantrips|cantrips known]]. If they don't appear on the [[Warlock Spell List#Cantrips|warlock spell list]], they are nonetheless warlock spells for you.
- If you lose your Book of Shadows, you can perform a 1-hour ceremony to receive a replacement from your patron. This ceremony can be performed during a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, and it destroys the previous book. The book turns to ash when you die.

### Pact of the Talisman

- Your patron gives you an amulet, a talisman that can aid the wearer when the need is great. When the wearer fails an [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]], they can add a d4 to the roll, potentially turning the roll into a success. This benefit can be used a number of times equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]], and all expended uses are restored when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]].
- If you lose the talisman, you can perform a 1-hour ceremony to receive a replacement from your patron. This ceremony can be performed during a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, and it destroys the previous amulet. The talisman turns to ash when you die.

### Pact of the Star Chain (UA)

_**Prerequisite: Seeker Patron**_

- The Seeker grants you a chain forged from starlight, decorated with seven gleaming motes of brightness. While the chain is on your person, you know the _[augury](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:augury)_ spell and can cast it as a ritual. The spell doesn't count against your number of spells known.
- Additionally, you can invoke the Seeker's power to gain advantage on an Intelligence check while you carry this item. Once you use this ability, you cannot use it again until you complete a short or long rest.
- If you lose your Star Chain, you can perform a 1-hour ceremony to receive a replacement from the Seeker. The ceremony can be performed during a short or long rest, and it destroys the previous chain. The chain disappears in a flash of light when you die.
- The exact form of this item might be different depending on your patron. The Star Chain is inspired by the Greyhawk deity Celestian.

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Eldritch Versatility (Optional)

Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can do one of the following, representing a change of focus in your occult studies:

- Replace one [[Warlock#Cantrips|cantrip]] you learned from this class's Pact Magic feature with another [[Warlock Spell List#Cantrips|cantrip from the warlock spell list]].

- Replace the option you chose for the [[Warlock#Pact Boon|Pact Boon feature]] with one of that feature's other options.

- If you're 12th level or higher, replace one spell from your [[Warlock#Mystic Arcanum|Mystic Arcanum feature]] with another [[Warlock Spell List|warlock spell]] of the same level.

If this change makes you ineligible for any of your [[Eldritch Invocations]], you must also replace them now, choosing invocations for which you qualify.

## Mystic Arcanum

At 11th level, your patron bestows upon you a magical secret called an arcanum. Choose one 6th-level spell from the [[Warlock Spell List|warlock spell list]] as this arcanum.

You can cast your arcanum spell once without expending a spell slot. You must finish a long rest before you can do so again.

At higher levels, you gain more warlock spells of your choice that can be cast in this way: one 7th-level spell at 13th level, one 8th-level spell at 15th level, and one 9th-level spell at 17th level. You regain all uses of your Mystic Arcanum when you finish a long rest.

## Eldritch Master

At 20th level, you can draw on your inner reserve of mystical power while entreating your patron to regain expended spell slots. You can spend 1 minute entreating your patron for aid to regain all your expended spell slots from your [[Warlock#Pact Magic|Pact Magic feature]]. Once you regain spell slots with this feature, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] before you can do so again.
