### Blood Curse of the Anxious

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you harry the body or mind of a creature within 30 feet of you, making them susceptible to forceful influence. Until the end of your next turn, [[Ability Scores#Charisma Checks|Charisma (Intimidation) checks]] made against the cursed creature have advantage.

**_Amplify._** The next Wisdom [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] the cursed creature makes before this curse ends has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].

### Blood Curse of Binding

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you attempt to bind a [[Combat Rules#Size Category Table|Large or smaller]] creature you can see within 30 feet of you, which must make a Strength [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]]. On a failure, the cursed creature’s [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] is reduced to 0 and it can’t use [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] until the end of your next turn.

**_Amplify._** This curse lasts for 1 minute and can affect any creature regardless of size. The cursed creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success.

### Blood Curse of Bloated Agony

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you curse a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you, causing its body to swell until the end of your next turn. For the duration, the creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength checks]] and [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity checks]], and takes 1d8 necrotic damage if it makes more than one [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack during]] its turn.

**_Amplify._** This curse lasts for 1 minute. The cursed creature can make a Constitution [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success.

### Blood Curse of Corrosion

**_Prerequisite: 15th level, Order of the Mutant_**\
As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you cause a creature within 30 feet of you to become [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]]. The cursed creature can make a Constitution [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] at the end of each of its turns, ending the curse on itself on a success.

**_Amplify._** The cursed creature takes 4d6 necrotic damage when you inflict this curse, and it takes this damage again each time it fails a Constitution saving throw to end the curse.

### Blood Curse of the Exorcist

**_Prerequisite: 15th level, Order of the Ghostslayer_**\
As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you that is [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], or which is under a possession effect. The target creature is no longer charmed, frightened, or possessed.

**_Amplify._** A creature that charmed, frightened, or possessed the target of your curse takes 3d6 psychic damage and must succeed on a Wisdom [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] or be stunned until the end of your next turn.

### Blood Curse of Exposure

When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage from an attack or spell, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to temporarily weaken its resilience. Until the end of the target’s next turn, it loses [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to all the damage types dealt by the triggering attack or spell (including for that triggering effect).

**_Amplify._** The target instead loses invulnerability to the damage types of the triggering attack or spell, but has resistance to those damage types until the end of its next turn.

### Blood Curse of the Eyeless

When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]], you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to roll one [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] and subtract the number rolled from the creature’s attack roll. You can choose to use this feature after the creature’s roll, but before the DM determines whether the attack hits or misses. The creature is immune to this curse if it is immune to the [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded condition]].

**_Amplify._** You apply this curse to all the creature’s [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] until the end of the creature’s turn. You roll separately for each affected attack.

### Blood Curse of the Fallen Puppet

When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to instill that creature with a final act of aggression. The creature immediately makes one [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|weapon attack]] against a target of your choice within its range.

**_Amplify._** You can first cause the cursed creature to move up to half its [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]], and you grant a bonus to its attack roll equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|Hemocraft modifier]] (minimum of +1).

### Blood Curse of the Howl

**_Prerequisite: 18th level, Order of the Lycan_**\
As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you unleash a bloodcurdling howl. Each creature within 30 feet of you that can hear you must succeed on a Wisdom [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] or become [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] of you until the end of your next turn. If a creature fails its saving throw by 5 or more, it is [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] while frightened in this way. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw is immune to this blood curse for the next 24 hours.

You can choose any number of creatures you can see to be unaffected by the howl.

**_Amplify._** The range of this curse increases to 60 feet.

### Blood Curse of the Marked

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you mark a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. Until the end of your turn, whenever you hit the cursed creature with a weapon for which you have an active [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|crimson rite]], you roll an additional [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] when determining the extra damage from the rite.

**_Amplify._** The next [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]] you make against the target before the end of your turn has advantage.

### Blood Curse of the Muddled Mind

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you curse a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you that is [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentrating]] on a spell or using a feature that requires concentration. That creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on the next Constitution [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] it makes to maintain concentration before the end of your next turn.

**_Amplify._** The cursed creature has disadvantage on all Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration until the end of your next turn.

### Blood Curse of the Soul Eater

**_Prerequisite: 18th level, Order of the Profane Soul_**\
When a creature that isn’t a [[Monster Rules#Type|construct or undead]] is reduced to 0 hit points within 30 feet of you, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to offer their life energy to your patron in exchange for power. Until the end of your next turn, you make [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attacks]] with [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] and you have [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to all damage.

**_Amplify._** Additionally, you regain an expended warlock spell slot. Once you’ve amplified this blood curse, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] before you can amplify it again.
