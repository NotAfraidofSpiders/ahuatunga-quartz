### Wild Magic Surge

Starting when you choose this origin at 1st level, your spellcasting can unleash surges of untamed magic. Once per turn, the DM can have you roll a d20 immediately after you cast a [[Sorcerer#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|sorcerer spell]] of 1st level or higher. If you roll a 1, roll on the [[Wild Magic Origin#Wild Magic Surge Table|Wild Magic Surge table]] to create a magical effect. If that effect is a spell, it is too wild to be affected by your [[Sorcerer#font of magic|Metamagic]], and if it normally requires [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], it doesn't require concentration in this case; the spell lasts for its full duration.

### Tides of Chaos

Starting at 1st level, you can manipulate the forces of chance and chaos to gain [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on one [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]], or [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]]. Once you do so, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] before you can use this feature again. ^991f33

Any time before you regain the use of this feature, the DM can have you roll on the [[Wild Magic Origin#Wild Magic Surge Table|Wild Magic Surge table]] immediately after you cast a [[Sorcerer#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|sorcerer spell]] of 1st level or higher. You then regain the use of this feature. ^9e90f3

### Bend Luck

Starting at 6th level, you have the ability to twist fate using your wild magic. When another creature you can see makes an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]], an [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability checks]], or a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]], you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] and spend 2 [[Sorcerer#font of magic|sorcery points]] to roll 1d4 and apply the number rolled as a bonus or penalty (your choice) to the creature's roll. You can do so after the creature rolls but before any effects of the roll occur.

### Controlled Chaos

At 14th level, you gain a modicum of control over the surges of your wild magic. Whenever you roll on the [[Wild Magic Origin#Wild Magic Surge Table|Wild Magic Surge table]], you can roll twice and use either number.

### Spell Bombardment

Beginning at 18th level, the harmful energy of your spells intensifies. When you roll damage for a spell and roll the highest number possible on any of the dice, choose one of those dice, roll it again and add that roll to the damage. You can use the feature only once per turn.
