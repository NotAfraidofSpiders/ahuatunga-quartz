# Class Features

As a wizard, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d6 per wizard level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 6 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]]
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d6 (or 4) + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]] per wizard level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** None\
**Weapons:** [[Weapons#Dagger|Daggers]], [[Weapons#Dart|darts]], [[Weapons#Sling|slings]], [[Weapons#Quarterstaff|quarterstaffs]], [[Weapons#Light Crossbow|light crossbows]]\
**Tools:** None\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Intelligence, Wisdom]]\
**Skills:** Choose two from [[Ability Scores#Skills|Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, and Religion]]

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a [[Weapons#Quarterstaff|quarterstaff]] or (b) a [[Weapons#Dagger|dagger]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Component Pouch|component pouch]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Arcane Focus|arcane focus]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Scholar's Pack|scholar's pack]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Explorer's Pack|explorer's pack]]
- A [[Adventuring Gear#Spellbook|spellbook]]

## Spellcasting

As a student of arcane magic, you have a spellbook containing spells that show the first glimmerings of your true power.

### Cantrips

At 1st level, you know three [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrips]] of your choice from the [[Wizard Spell List]]. You learn additional [[Wizard Spell List#Cantrips|wizard cantrips]] of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the [[Wizard#Wizard Progression Table|Wizard table]].

### Spellbook

At 1st level, you have a [[Adventuring Gear#Spellbook|spellbook]] containing six 1st-level wizard spells of your choice. Your spellbook is the repository of the [[Wizard#Learning Spells of 1st Level and Higher|wizard spells you know]], except your [[Wizard#Cantrips|cantrips]], which are fixed in your mind.

The spells that you add to your spellbook as you gain levels reflect the arcane research you conduct on your own, as well as intellectual breakthroughs you have had about the nature of the multiverse. You might find other spells during your adventures. You could discover a spell recorded on a scroll in an evil wizard's chest, for example, or in a dusty tome in an ancient library.

#### Copying a Spell into the Book 

When you find a wizard spell of 1st level or higher, you can add it to your spellbook if it is of a [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|spell level]] you can [[Wizard#Preparing and Casting Spells|prepare]] and if you can spare the time to decipher and copy it.

Copying a spell into your spellbook involves reproducing the basic form of the spell, then deciphering the unique system of notation used by the wizard who wrote it. You must practice the spell until you understand the sounds or gestures required, then transcribe it into your spellbook using your own notation.

For each [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|level of the spell]], the process takes 2 hours and costs 50 gp. The cost represents [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|material components]] you expend as you experiment with the spell to master it, as well as the fine inks you need to record it. Once you have spent this time and money, you can [[Wizard#Preparing and Casting Spells|prepare the spell just like your other spells]].

#### Replacing the Book 

You can [[Wizard#Copying a Spell into the Book|copy a spell]] from your own [[Adventuring Gear#Spellbook|spellbook]] into another [[Adventuring Gear#Book|book]]-for example, if you want to make a backup copy of your spellbook. This is just like copying a new spell into your spellbook, but faster and easier, since you understand your own notation and already know how to cast the spell. You need spend only 1 hour and 10 gp for each level of the copied spell.

If you lose your spellbook, you can use the same procedure to transcribe the spells that you have prepared into a new spellbook. Filling out the remainder of your spellbook requires you to find new spells to do so, as normal. For this reason, many wizards keep backup spellbooks in a safe place.

#### The Book's Appearance 

Your [[Adventuring Gear#Spellbook|spellbook]] is a unique compilation of spells, with its own decorative flourishes and margin notes. It might be a plain, functional leather volume that you received as a gift from your master, a finely bound gilt-edged tome you found in an ancient library or even a loose collection of notes scrounged together after you lost your previous spellbook in a mishap.

### Preparing and Casting Spells

The [[Wizard#Wizard Progression Table|Wizard table]] shows how many [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slots]] you have to cast your [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spells]] of 1st [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|level]] and higher. To [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast]] one of these spells, you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]].

You prepare the list of wizard spells that are available for you to cast. To do so, choose a number of wizard spells from your spellbook equal to your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers|Intelligence modifier]] + your wizard level (minimum of one spell). The spells must be of a [[Wizard#Wizard Progression Table|level for which you have spell slots]].

For example, if you're a 3rd-level wizard, you have four 1st-level and two 2nd-level spell slots. With an [[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intelligence]] of 16, your list of prepared spells can include six spells of 1st or 2nd level, in any combination, chosen from your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]]. If you prepare the 1st-level spell _[[Spells#Magic Missile|magic missile]]_, you can cast it using a 1st-level or a 2nd-level slot. Casting the spell doesn't remove it from your list of prepared spells.

You can change your list of prepared spells when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]]. Preparing a new list of [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spells]] requires time spent studying your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]] and memorizing the incantations and gestures you must make to [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast the spell]]: at least 1 minute per [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|spell level]] for each spell on your list.

### Spellcasting Ability

[[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intelligence]] is your spellcasting ability for your [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spells]], since you learn your spells through dedicated study and memorization. You use your [[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intelligence]] whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]] when setting the [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] DC for a [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spell]] you cast and when making an [[Spellcasting Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]] with one.

**Spell save DC** = 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]]

**Spell attack modifier** = your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]]

### Ritual Casting

You can cast a [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spell]] as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Rituals|ritual]] if that spell has the ritual tag and you have the spell in your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]]. You don't need to have the spell [[Wizard#Preparing and Casting Spells|prepared]].

### Spellcasting Focus

You can use an [[Adventuring Gear#Arcane Focus|arcane]] focus as a spellcasting focus for your [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spells]].

### Learning Spells of 1st Level and Higher

Each time you gain a wizard level, you can add two [[Wizard Spell List|wizard spells]] of your choice to your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]]. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots, as shown on the [[Wizard#Wizard Progression Table|Wizard table]]. On your [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]], you might find other spells that you can add to your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]].

## Arcane Recovery

You have learned to regain some of your magical energy by studying your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]]. Once per day when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short rest]], you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slots]] can have a combined level that is equal to or less than half your wizard level (rounded up), and none of the slots can be 6th level or higher. ^55e78f

For example, if you're a 4th-level wizard, you can recover up to two levels worth of spell slots. You can recover either a 2nd-level spell slot or two 1st-level spell slots.

## Cantrip Formulas (Optional)

At 3rd level, you have scribed a set of arcane formulas in your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]] that you can use to formulate a [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrip]] in your mind. Whenever you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] and consult those formulas in your spellbook, you can replace one [[Wizard Spell List#Cantrips|wizard cantrip]] you know with another cantrip from the wizard spell list.

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Spell Mastery

At 18th level, you have achieved such mastery over certain spells that you can cast them at will. Choose a [[Wizard Spell List#1st Level Spells|1st-level wizard spell]] and a [[Wizard Spell List#2nd Level Wizard Spells|2nd-level wizard spell]] that are in your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]]. You can cast those spells at their lowest level without expending a spell slot when you have them prepared. If you want to cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot as normal.

By spending 8 hours in study, you can exchange one or both of the spells you chose for different spells of the same levels.

## Signature Spells

When you reach 20th level, you gain mastery over two powerful spells and can cast them with little effort. Choose two [[Wizard Spell List#3rd Level Wizard Spells|3rd-level wizard spells]] in your [[Wizard#Spellbook|spellbook]] as your signature spells. You always have these spells [[Wizard#Preparing and Casting Spells|prepared]], they don't count against the number of spells you have prepared, and you can cast each of them once at 3rd level without expending a spell slot. When you do so, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest.

If you want to cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot as normal.
