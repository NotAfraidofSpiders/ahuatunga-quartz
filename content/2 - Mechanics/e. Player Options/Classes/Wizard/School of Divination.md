### Divination Savant

Beginning when you [[Wizard#Arcane Tradition|select this school at 2nd level]], the gold and time you must spend to [[Wizard#Copying a Spell into the Book|copy a Divination spell into your spellbook]] is halved.

### Portent

Starting at 2nd level when you choose this school, glimpses of the future begin to press in on your awareness. When you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]], roll two d20s and record the numbers rolled. You can replace any [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]], or [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll, and you can replace a roll in this way only once per turn.

Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]], you lose any unused foretelling rolls.

### Expert Divination

Beginning at 6th level, casting [[Spellcasting Rules#Divination|divination spells]] comes so easily to you that it expends only a fraction of your spellcasting efforts. When you cast a divination spell of 2nd level or higher using a [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slot]], you regain one expended spell slot. The slot you regain must be of a level lower than the spell you cast and can't be higher than 5th level.

### The Third Eye

Starting at 10th level, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Actions in Combat|action]] to increase your powers of perception. When you do so, choose one of the following benefits, which lasts until you are incapacitated or you take a short or long rest. You can't use the feature again until you finish a short or long rest.

**_Darkvision._** You gain [[Environment Rules#Darkvision|darkvision]] out to a range of 60 feet.

**_Ethereal Sight._** You can see into the Ethereal Plane within 60 feet of you.

**_Greater Comprehension._** You can read any language.

**_See Invisibility._** You can see [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects within 10 feet of you that are within line of sight.

### Greater Portent

Starting at 14th level, the visions in your dreams intensify and paint a more accurate picture in your mind of what is to come. You roll three d20s for your [[School of Divination#Portent|Portent feature]], rather than two.
