_You must have an Intelligence score of 13 or higher and a Strength or Dexterity score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class._

_If your blood hunter is part of the Order of the Profane Soul and also has warlock levels, add one-third of your blood hunter levels (rounded down) to your warlock level and consult the [[Warlock#Warlock Progression Table|warlock progression table]] for total spell slots, cantrips known, and spell slot level. You should consider aligning your Otherworldly Patron feature between both classes, but your DM might allow you to have two different patrons at their discretion._

**_Variant Hemocraft Ability Score_**: As a blood hunter, you use your Intelligence modifier for some of your class and subclass features. However, with your DM’s permission, you can choose to instead use your Wisdom modifier for all your blood hunter features that use your Intelligence modifier by default.

# Class Features

As a blood hunter, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d10 per blood hunter level\
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]]\
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]] per blood hunter level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** [[Armor Rules#Light Armor|Light armor]], [[Armor Rules#Medium Armor|medium armor]], [[Armor#Shield|shields]]\
**Weapons:** [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Simple weapons, martial weapons]]\
**Tools:** [[Tools#Artisan’s Tools|Alchemist's supplies]]\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Dexterity, Intelligence]]\
**Skills:** Choose three from [[Ability Scores#Skills|Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, History, Insight, Investigation, Religion, and Survival]]

### Equipment

- (a) a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|martial weapon]] or (b) two [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple weapons]]
- (a) a [[Weapons#Light Crossbow|light crossbow]] and [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|20 bolts]] or (b) [[Weapons#Hand Crossbow|hand crossbow]] and [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|20 bolts]]
- (a) [[Armor#Studded Leather|studded leather armor]] or (b) [[Armor#Scale Mail|scale mail armor]]
- an [[Adventuring Gear#Explorer's Pack|explorer's pack]] and [[Tools#Artisan’s Tools|alchemist's supplies]]

## Hunter's Bane

At 1st level, you have survived the Hunter’s Bane—a dangerous, long-guarded ritual that alters your life’s blood, forever binding you to the darkness and honing your senses against it.

You have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Wisdom Checks|Wisdom (Survival) checks]] to track [[Monster Rules#Type|fey, fiends, or undead]], as well as on [[Ability Scores#Intelligence Checks|Intelligence checks]] to recall information about such creatures. ^877b04

The Hunter’s Bane also empowers your body to control and shape hemocraft magic, using your own blood and life essence to fuel your abilities. Some of your features require your target to make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] to resist the feature’s effects. The saving throw DC is calculated as follows: ^4d6700

**Hemocraft save DC** = 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your Hemocraft modifier (your choice between [[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intelligence]] and [[Ability Scores#Wisdom|Wisdom]]) ^3639aa

## Blood Maledict

Also at 1st level, you gain the ability to channel—or sometimes sacrifice—a part of your vital essence to curse and manipulate creatures through hemocraft magic. You know one [[Blood Curses|blood curse]] of your choice. You learn one additional blood curse of your choice at 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th level. Each time you learn a new blood curse, you can also choose one of the blood curses you know and replace it with another blood curse.

Each time you use your Blood Maledict feature, you choose which curse to invoke from the curses you know. While invoking a blood curse, but before it affects the target, you can choose to amplify the curse by taking necrotic damage equal to one roll of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]]. This damage can’t be reduced in any way. An amplified curse gains an additional effect, noted in the curse’s description. Creatures that do not have blood are immune to blood curses unless you have amplified the curse. ^459079

Once you use this feature, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest before you can use it again. You can use Blood Maledict twice between rests starting at 6th level, three times starting at 13th level, and four times starting at 17th level. ^a60c49

## Crimson Rite

Also at 2nd level, you learn to invoke a rite of hemocraft that infuses your weapon strikes with elemental energy.

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you can activate any rite you know on one [[Weapon Rules|weapon]] you’re holding. The effect of the rite lasts until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest. When you activate a rite, you take necrotic damage equal to one roll of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]]. This damage can’t be reduced in any way. ^cb5df9

While the rite is in effect, [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attacks]] you make with this weapon are magical, and deal extra damage equal to your hemocraft die of the type determined by the [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rites|chosen rite]]. A weapon can hold only one active rite at a time. Other creatures can’t gain the benefit of your rite. ^1a835e

You choose one rite from the crimson rites below when you first gain this feature. You learn an additional crimson rite at 7th level, and again at 14th level.

## Crimson Rites

#### Rite of the Flame

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is fire damage.

#### Rite of the Frozen

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is cold damage.

#### Rite of the Storm

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is lightning damage.

#### Rite of the Dead

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is necrotic damage. (Prerequisite: 14th level)

#### Rite of the Oracle

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is psychic damage. (Prerequisite: 14th level)

#### Rite of the Roar

Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite]] damage is thunder damage. (Prerequisite: 14th level)

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can’t increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

Using the optional feats rule, you can forgo taking this feature to take a feat of your choice instead.

## Extra Attack

Starting at 5th level, you can [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] twice, instead of once, whenever you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]] on your turn.

## Brand of Castigation

At 6th level, when you damage a creature with a weapon for which you have an active [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|crimson rite]], you can channel hemocraft magic to sear an arcane brand into that creature (no action required). You always know the direction to the branded creature as long as it’s on the same plane as you. Further, each time the branded creature deals damage to you or a creature you can see within 5 feet of you, the branded creature takes psychic damage equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|Hemocraft modifier]] (minimum of 1).

Your brand lasts until you dismiss it or until you use this feature to apply a brand to another creature. Your brand can be dispelled with [[Spells#Dispel Magic|Dispel Magic]], and is treated as a spell with a level equal to half your blood hunter level (maximum 9th level).

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest.

## Grim Psychometry

When you reach 9th level, you gain a supernatural talent for discerning the secrets surrounding mysterious relics or places touched by evil. Whenever you make an [[Ability Scores#Intelligence Checks|Intelligence (History) check]] to recall information about the sinister or tragic history of an object you are touching or your current location, you have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on the check. At the DM’s discretion, a suitably high roll might cause your character to experience brief visions of the past connected to the object or location.

## Dark Augmentation

Starting at 10th level, the magic of hemocraft suffuses your body to permanently reinforce your resilience. Your [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] increases by 5 feet, and you have a bonus to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|Hemocraft modifier]] (minimum of +1).

## Brand of Tethering

Starting at 13th level, the psychic damage from your [[Blood Hunter#Brand of Castigation|Brand of Castigation]] increases to twice your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|Hemocraft modifier]] (minimum of 2). Additionally, a branded creature can’t take the [[Combat Rules#Dash|Dash]] action, and if it attempts to teleport or to leave its current plane by any means, it takes 4d6 psychic damage and must make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throws]]. On a failure, the attempt to teleport or leave the plane fails.

## Hardened Soul

When you reach 14th level, you have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] against being [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] and [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]].

## Sanguine Mastery

Upon reaching 20th level, your mastery of blood magic reaches its height, mitigating your sacrifice and empowering your expertise. Once per turn, whenever a blood hunter feature requires you to roll a [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]], you can reroll the die and use either roll.

Additionally, whenever you score a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] with a [[Weapon Rules|weapon]] for which you have an active [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|crimson rite]], you regain one expended use of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Maledict|blood maledict]] feature.
