# Rite of the Plague

Beginning at 3rd level, you learn the Rite of the Plague (detailed below)

- Your [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|rite damage]] is poison damage, additionally while your rite is active, whenever you hit a creature with your rite weapon, if that attack had advantage the creature becomes [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] until the start of your next turn.
- When you reach 7th level, while this rite is active you ignore [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]]  to poison damage and immunity to the poisoned condition.

# Pestilent Disruption

Also at 3rd level you learn the "[[Order of the Virulent Magistrate#Swarm (3rd)|Swarm]]” Disruption, and one other [[Order of the Virulent Magistrate#Disruption Options|Disruption]] of your choice. In order to use a pestilent disruption you must end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption. To learn a pestilent disruption you must meet any prerequisites it has. You learn more disruptions at certain levels in this class. You know 4 Disruptions at 7th level, 5 at 11th level, 7 at 15th level, and 8 at 18th level.

## Disruption Options

### Swarm (3rd)

As an action you end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption to conjure a swarm of vermin. When you use this disruption choose buzzing, wriggling, or gnashing. A [[Order of the Virulent Magistrate#Fetid Swarm Stat block|fetid swarm]] of the type you chose appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. The swarm consists of insects, vermin, or similarly small creatures. ^ac2c4b

On your turn you can use a bonus action to issue a verbal command to the swarm to take an action in its stat block or [[Combat Rules#Actions in Combat|another action]] that is not the [[Combat Rules#Help|help]] action. Your commands can be in any language you speak or some variation of chirping or chirruping unique to your verminous companions. If you issue no commands to the swarm it defends itself but otherwise takes no action.  ^4a5c9b

The swarm lasts until you take a short or long rest or until you dismiss it as an action. ^f3fddd

### Skulk (3rd)

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] you end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption to mix your essence with gloom for one hour.  ^59b07c

When you activate this disruption and on subsequent turns for the duration, while within [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]] you can take the [[Combat Rules#Hide|hide action]] as a bonus action. You can hide in this way a number of times equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]].  ^41cb68

Additionally, when you or a creature you can see within 15 feet of you hits a creature that is [[Combat Rules#Unseen Attackers and Targets|unaware of the attacker]], you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to cause the target of the attack to become [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] until the end of its next turn. ^e46a27

### Mesmerize (3rd)

As an action you end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption to call forth a transfixing spirit of filth, which appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet of you.

When the spirit appears, choose any number of creatures up to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency modifier]] within 10 feet of the spirit. Those creatures must succeed on a wisdom [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]]. On a failure a creature becomes [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] by you until the end of your next turn or until it takes damage.

While charmed in this way a creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]].  

### Fumigate (3rd)

When you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|attack action]], instead of attacking, you may end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption to produce a 20 ft [[Spellcasting Rules#Cone|cone]] of noxious fumes originating from you.

Each creature in the cone has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on Constitution saving throws for 1 minute and takes poison damage equal to two rolls of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]].

### Briars (7th)

When you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|attack action]], instead of attacking, you may end one [[Blood Hunter#Crimson Rite|active rite]] or an ongoing pestilent disruption to ensnare and capture your assailants. Each creature of your choice within 15 ft. of you takes piercing damage equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] + your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]] and must succeed on strength saving throw or become [[Conditions#Grappled|grappled]] for one minute as vines, vermin, and pests attempt to bind them in place.

A creature that fails this save by five or more is also [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] for the duration.

A grappled creature takes piercing damage equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] + your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]] and may repeat the strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Clamor (7th)

As an action, you incite a deafening cacophony, within a 40 ft radius sphere which spreads around corners centered on a point you can see within 90 feet of you. The area of the sphere is [[Environment Rules#Lightly Obscured|lightly obscured]], and creatures within it are [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]]. Any creature with special senses such as [[Environment Rules#Tremorsense|tremorsense]] or [[Environment Rules#Blindsight|blindsight]] that relies on hearing cannot use that sense while deafened in this way. The clamor lasts for 10 minutes, or until you end it as a bonus action.

### Molt (7th)

As a reaction which you take when you are dealt damage, you gain [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to one type of the inciting damage and regain a number of hit-points equal to 2 rolls of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]]. Additionally, if you were dealt damage by a creature, that creature becomes [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] for 1 minute. At the end of each of the poisoned creatures turns, it may make a constitution saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Displace (11th)

Replacing one attack you make using the attack action you attempt to teleport a creature you can touch to a point you can see within 30 feet of you. To teleport an unwilling creature, make a melee spell attack using your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft ability]] hemocraft ability. On a hit, the creature is teleported as normal. If the point you choose is occupied by a creature or object, both the creature you teleported and the creature or object at the destination take 1d10 force damage.

### Eye (11th)

As a bonus action you augment your senses with those of the mites and vermin which otherwise go unnoticed, granting you certain benefits. For 1 minute, you have advantage on perception and investigation checks and are immune to the [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] condition. Additionally, creatures within 15 feet of you [[Ability Scores#Hiding|cannot be hidden]] from you and cannot benefit from being [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]]. The first attack roll you make on each of your turns for the duration has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]]. While your senses are heightened in this way, whenever a creature within 15 feet of you becomes the target of an attack or makes a saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]] to its AC or saving throw respectively.

### Feast (11th)

As a bonus action you begin to prey upon the suffering of your enemies. For 1 minute, whenever a hostile creature you can see within 30 feet of you becomes [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]], [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]], [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]], [[Conditions#Petrified#petrified|petrified]], [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]], or becomes unable to regain hit points, you gain temporary hit points equal to one roll of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] + your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]]

# Putrid Charms

Beginning when you reach 7th level, as long as a friendly creature occupies the same space as your [[Order of the Virulent Magistrate#Fetid Swarm Stat block|fetid swarm]], that creature has half cover and both you and that creature ignore resistance to poison damage and immunity to the [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]] condition. Additionally, when either you or a friendly creature in the same space as your swarm hit with a weapon attack, the attack deals additional poison damage equal to one roll of your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]]. 

# Brand of the Infected

Starting at 11th level, your [[Blood Hunter#Brand of Castigation|Brand of Castigation]] infects a creature with infectious oozing pustules, which give it [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Charisma Checks|charisma checks]] while branded. While branded, each creature friendly to the branded creature that starts its turn within 15 feet it takes poison damage equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Hunter's Bane|hemocraft modifier]]. Additionally, when the branded creature would take poison damage, its hit point maximum is instead reduced by an equivalent amount which can’t be restored until it is no longer branded or magic such as _[[Spells#greater restoration|greater restoration]]_ or _[[Spells#Remove Curse|remove curse]]_ spell are cast on it. 

# Blood Curse of the Leech

At 15th level you learn the [[Blood Hunter#Blood Maledict|Blood Curse]] of the Leech Described below:

As an action, you curse a creature within 30 feet of you for 1 minute. Creating a tether between that creature and a creature of your choice, whenever the cursed creature is dealt damage by the tethered creature its life essence is siphoned from its body, healing the tethered creature for half of the damage taken. The affected creature can make a constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on it on a success. 

---

**Amplify**: The tethered creature instead heals whenever it is dealt damage by any source not only their own effects.

# Bottom Feeder

When you reach 18th level, whenever you activate a Crimson Rite, grant a creature of your choice that you can see creature within 10 feet of you or your swarms temporary hit-points equal to your [[Blood Hunter#Blood Hunter Progression Table|hemocraft die]] + half your blood hunter level and end one of the following conditions affecting the target: [[Conditions#Poisoned|poisoned]], [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]], [[Conditions#Deafened|deafened]], [[Conditions#Petrified|petrified]], [[Conditions#Paralyzed|paralyzed]], or [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]].
