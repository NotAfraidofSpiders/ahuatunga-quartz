# Class Features

As a fighter, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d10 per fighter level\
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier\
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per fighter level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** All [[Armor Rules#Armor Table|armor, shields]]\
**Weapons:** [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Simple weapons, martial weapons]]\
**Tools:** None\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Strength, Constitution]]\
**Skills:** Choose two skills from [[Ability Scores#Skills|Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, and Survival]]

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) [[Armor#Chain Mail|chain mail]] or (b) [[Armor#Leather Armor|leather]], [[Weapons#Longbow|longbow]], and 20 [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|arrows]]
- (a) a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|martial weapon]] and a [[Armor#Shield|shield]] or (b) [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|two martial weapons]]
- (a) a [[Weapons#Light Crossbow|light crossbow]] and 20 [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|bolts]] or (b) two [[Weapons#Handaxe|handaxes]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Dungeoneer's Pack|dungeoneer's pack]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Explorer's Pack|explorer's pack]]

## Fighting Style

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options. You can't take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again.

### Archery

You gain a +2 bonus to [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] you make with [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged weapons]].

### Blind Fighting (TCE)

You have [[Environment Rules#Blindsight|blindsight]] with a range of 10 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]] or in [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]]. Moreover, you can see an [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creature within that range, unless the creature successfully [[Ability Scores#Hiding|hides]] from you.

### Defense

While you are wearing [[Armor Rules|armor]], you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

### Dueling

When you are wielding a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|melee weapon]] in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to [[Combat Rules#Damage Rolls|damage rolls]] with that weapon.

### Great Weapon Fighting

When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|attack]] you make with a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|melee weapon]] that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2. The weapon must have the [[Weapon Rules#Two-Handed|two-handed]] or [[Weapon Rules#Versatile|versatile]] property for you to gain this benefit.

### Interception

When a creature you can see hits a target, other than you, within 5 feet of you with an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]], you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to reduce the damage the target takes by 1d10 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] (to a minimum of 0 damage). You must be wielding a [[Armor#Shield|shield]] or a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple or martial weapon]] to use this reaction.

### Protection

When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on the [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]]. You must be wielding a [[Armor#Shield|shield]].

### Superior Technique

You learn one [maneuver](http://dnd5e.wikidot.com/fighter:battle-master:maneuvers) of your choice from among those available to the [Battle Master](http://dnd5e.wikidot.com/fighter:battle-master) archetype. If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] to resist the maneuver's effects, the saving throw DC equals 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Strength|Strength]] or [[Ability Scores#Dexterity|Dexterity]] modifier (your choice.)

- You gain one superiority die, which is a d6 (this die is added to any superiority dice you have from another source). This die is used to fuel your maneuvers. A superiority die is expended when you use it. You regain your expended superiority dice when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest.

### Thrown Weapon Fighting

You can draw a weapon that has the [[Weapon Rules#Thrown|thrown property]] as part of the attack you make with the weapon.

- In addition, when you hit with a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] using a thrown weapon, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.

### Two-Weapon Fighting

When you engage in [[Combat Rules#Two-Weapon Fighting|two-weapon fighting]], you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Unarmed Fighting

Your [[Combat Rules#Unarmed strike|unarmed strikes]] can deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d6 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Strength modifier]] on a hit. If you aren't wielding any [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|weapons]] or a [[Armor#Shield|shield]] when you make the [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], the d6 becomes a d8.

- At the start of each of your turns, you can deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage to one creature [[Combat Rules#Grappling|grappled]] by you.

### Close Quarters Shooter

When making a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] while you are within 5 feet of a hostile creature, you do not have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on the [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]]. Your ranged attacks ignore [[Combat Rules#Cover|half cover and three-quarters cover]] against targets within 30 feet of you. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls on ranged attacks.

### Mariner (UA)

As long as you are not wearing [[Armor Rules#Heavy Armor|heavy armor]] or using a [[Armor#Shield|shield]], you have a [[Monster Rules#Swim|swimming speed]] and a [[Monster Rules#Climb|climbing speed]] equal to your normal [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]], and you gain a +1 bonus to armor class. ^8df135

#### Homebrew Revision:

- You only gain the climbing speed if you are 8th level or higher. ^ec517d

### Tunnel Fighter (UA)

As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you can enter a defensive stance that lasts until the start of your next turn. While in your defensive stance, you can make [[Combat Rules#Opportunity Attacks|opportunity attacks]] without using your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]], and you can use your reaction to make a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee attack]] against a creature that moves more than 5 feet while within your reach.

## Second Wind

You have a limited well of stamina that you can draw on to protect yourself from harm. On your turn, you can use a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] to regain hit points equal to 1d10 + your fighter level. ^10ec3b

Once you use this feature, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest before you can use it again. ^f9d392

## Action Surge

Starting at 2nd level, you can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]]. ^a81e75

Once you use this feature, you must finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest before you can use it again. Starting at 17th level, you can use it twice before a rest, but only once on the same turn. ^ed994d

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 6th, 8th, 12th, 14th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Martial Versatility (Optional)

Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the [[Fighter#Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement feature]], you can do one of the following, as you shift the focus of your martial practice: ^048cc7

- Replace a [[Fighter#Fighting Style|fighting style]] you know with another fighting style available to fighters.
  ^fa4fd7
- If you know any maneuvers from the Battle Master archetype, you can replace one maneuver you know with a different maneuver. ^3ca125

## Extra Attack

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the [[Combat Rules#Attack|Attack action]] on your turn.

The number of attacks increases to three when you reach 11th level in this class and to four when you reach 20th level in this class.

## Indomitable

Beginning at 9th level, you can reroll a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] that you fail. If you do so, you must use the new roll, and you can't use this feature again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]].

You can use this feature twice between long rests starting at 13th level and three times between [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rests]] starting at 17th level.
