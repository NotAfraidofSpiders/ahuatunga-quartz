**_Awake to the psionic power within, a Psi Warrior is a fighter who augments their physical might with psi-infused weapon strikes, telekinetic lashes, and barriers of mental force. Many githyanki train to become such warriors, as do some of the most disciplined high elves. In the world of Eberron, many young kalashtar dream of becoming Psi Warriors._**

**_As a Psi Warrior, you might have honed your psionic abilities through solo discipline, unlocked it under the tutelage of a master, or refined it at an academy dedicated to wielding the mind's power as both weapon and shield._**

Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

### Psionic Power

At 3rd level, you harbor a wellspring of psionic energy within yourself. This energy is represented by your Psionic Energy dice, which are each a d6. You have a number of these dice equal to twice your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]], and they fuel various psionic powers you have, which are detailed below. ^7b1edf

Some of your powers expend the Psionic Energy die they use, as specified in a power's description, and you can't use a power if it requires you to use a die when your dice are all expended. You regain all your expended Psionic Energy dice when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]]. In addition, as a bonus action, you can regain one expended Psionic Energy die, but you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest. ^287118

When you reach certain levels in this class, the size of your Psionic Energy dice increases: at 5th level (d8), 11th level (d10), and 17th level (d12).

The powers below use your Psionic Energy dice. ^0f8720

#### Protective Field

When you or another creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to expend one Psionic Energy die, roll the die, and reduce the damage taken by the number rolled plus your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]] (minimum reduction of 1), as you create a momentary shield of telekinetic force. ^7c26b2

#### Psionic Strike

You can propel your weapons with psionic force. Once on each of your turns, immediately after you hit a target within 30 feet of you with an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] and deal damage to it with a [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|weapon]], you can expend one [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]], rolling it and dealing force damage to the target equal to the number rolled plus your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]].

#### Telekinetic Movement

You can move an [[Object Rules|object]] or a creature with your mind. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you target one loose [[Object Rules|object]] that is [[Combat Rules#Size Category Table|Large]] or smaller or one willing creature, other than yourself. If you can see the target and it is within 30 feet of you, you can move it up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Alternatively, if it is a [[Combat Rules#Size Category Table|Tiny]] object, you can move it to or from your hand. Either way, you can move the target horizontally, vertically, or both. Once you take this action, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, unless you expend a [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]] to take it again. ^69d64b

### Telekinetic Adept

By the 7th level, You have mastered new ways to use your telekinetic abilities, detailed below.

_**Psi-Powered Leap.**_ As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you can propel your body with your mind. You gain a [[Monster Rules#Fly|flying speed]] equal to twice your walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] until the end of the current turn. Once you take this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest, unless you expend a [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]] to take it again.

_**Telekinetic Thrust.**_ When you deal damage to a target with your [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Strike|Psionic Strike]], you can force the target to make a [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Strength saving throw]] against a DC equal to 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]]. If the save fails, you can knock the target [[Conditions#Prone|prone]] or move it up to 10 feet in any direction horizontally.

### Guarded Mind

Starting at 10th level, the psionic energy flowing through you has bolstered your mind. You have [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to psychic damage. Moreover, if you start your turn [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] or [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]], you can expend a [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]] and end every effect on yourself subjecting you to those conditions.

### Bulwark of Force

At 15th level, you can shield yourself and others with telekinetic force. As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you can choose creatures, which can include you, that you can see within 30 feet of you, up to a number of creatures equal to your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Intelligence modifier]] (minimum of one creature). Each of the chosen creatures is protected by [[Combat Rules#Cover|half cover]] for 1 minute or until you're [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]].

Once you take this bonus action, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]], unless you expend a [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]] to take it again.

### Telekinetic Master

By 18th level, your ability to move creatures and objects with your mind is matched by few. You can cast the _[[Spells#Telekinesis|Telekinesis]]_ spell, requiring no [[Spellcasting Rules#Components|components]], and your spellcasting ability for the spell is [[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intelligence]]. On each of your turns while you [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentrate]] on the spell, including the turn when you cast it, you can make one [[Combat Rules#Making an Attack|attack]] with a weapon as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]].

Once you cast the spell with this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]], unless you expend a [[Psi Warrior#Psionic Power|Psionic Energy die]] to cast it again.
