# Class Features

As a bard, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per bard level\
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]]\
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per bard level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** [[Armor Rules#Light Armor|Light armor]]\
**Weapons:** [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|Simple weapons]], [[Weapons#Hand Crossbow|hand crossbows]], [[Weapons#Longsword|longswords]], [[Weapons#Rapier|rapiers]], [[Weapons#Shortsword|shortswords]]\
**Tools:** Three [[Tools#Musical Instrument|musical instruments]] of your choice\
**Saving Throws:** [[Ability Scores#Dexterity|Dexterity]], [[Ability Scores#Charisma|Charisma]]\
**Skills:** [[Ability Scores#Skills|Choose any three]]

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a [[Weapons#Rapier|rapier]], (b) a [[Weapons#Longsword|longsword]], or (c) any [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|simple weapon]]
- (a) a [[Adventuring Gear#Diplomat's Pack|diplomat's pack]] or (b) an [[Adventuring Gear#Entertainer's Pack|entertainer's pack]]
- (a) a lute or (b) any other [[Tools#Musical Instrument|musical instrument]]
- [[Armor#Leather Armor|Leather armor]] and a [[Weapons#Dagger|dagger]]

## Spellcasting

You have learned to untangle and reshape the fabric of reality in harmony with your wishes and music. Your spells are part of your vast repertoire, magic that you can tune to different situations.

### Cantrips

You know two [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrips]] of your choice from the [[Bard Spell List]]. You learn additional bard [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrips]] of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Bard table.

### Spell Slots

The [[Bard#Bard Progression Table|Bard Table]] shows how many [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slots]] you have to cast your bard spells of 1st level and higher. To [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast one of these spells]], you must expend a slot of the [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Level|spell's level]] or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]]. For example, if you know the 1st-level spell _[[Spells#Cure Wounds|cure wounds]]_ and have a 1st-level and a 2nd-level spell slot available, you can cast _[[Spells#Cure Wounds|cure wounds]]_ using either slot.

### Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher

You know four 1st-level spells of your choice from the [[Bard Spell List]].

The [[Spellcasting Rules#Known and Prepared Spells|Spells Known]] column of the [[Bard#Bard Progression Table|Bard Table]] shows when you learn more bard spells of your choice. Each of these spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots, as shown on the table. For instance, when you reach 3rd level in this class, you can learn one new spell of 1st or 2nd level.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the bard spells you know and replace it with another spell from the bard spell list, which also must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

### Spellcasting Ability

[[Ability Scores#Charisma|Charisma]] is your spellcasting ability for your [[Bard#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|bard spells]]. Your magic comes from the heart and soul you pour into the performance of your music or oration. You use your Charisma whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers|Charisma modifier]] when setting the [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] DC for a bard spell you cast and when making an [[Spellcasting Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]] with one.

**Spell save DC** = 8 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your Charisma [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|modifier]]

**Spell attack modifier** = your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] + your Charisma [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|modifier]]

### Ritual Casting

You can cast any [[Bard#Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher|bard spell you know]] as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Rituals|ritual]] if that spell has the ritual tag.

### Spellcasting Focus

You can use a [[Tools#Musical Instrument|musical instrument]] as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|spellcasting focus]] for your bard spells.

## Bardic Inspiration

You can inspire others through stirring words or music. To do so, you use a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] on your turn to choose one creature other than yourself within 60 feet of you who can hear you. That creature gains one Bardic Inspiration die, a d6. ^7c1bbc

Once within the next 10 minutes, the creature can roll the die and add the number rolled to one [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]], [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], or [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] it makes. The creature can wait until after it rolls the d20 before deciding to use the Bardic Inspiration die, but must decide before the DM says whether the roll succeeds or fails. Once the Bardic Inspiration die is rolled, it is lost. A creature can have only one Bardic Inspiration die at a time. ^b994bb

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Charisma modifier]] (a minimum of once). You regain any expended uses when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]]. ^9bd1f6

Your Bardic Inspiration die changes when you reach certain levels in this class. The die becomes a d8 at 5th level, a d10 at 10th level, and a d12 at 15th level. ^2d8efe

## Jack of All Trades

Starting at 2nd level, you can add half your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]], rounded down, to any [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] you make that doesn't already include your proficiency bonus. ^997ea7

## Song of Rest

Beginning at 2nd level, you can use soothing music or oration to help revitalize your wounded allies during a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short rest]]. If you or any friendly creatures who can hear your performance regain hit points at the end of the short rest by spending one or more Hit Dice, each of those creatures regains an extra 1d6 hit points. ^760fa2

The extra Hit Points increase when you reach certain levels in this class: to 1d8 at 9th level, to 1d10 at 13th level, and to 1d12 at 17th level. ^278b25

## Magical Inspiration (Optional)

At 2nd level, if a creature has a [[Bard#Bardic Inspiration|Bardic Inspiration die]] from you and casts a spell that restores hit points or deals damage, the creature can roll that die and choose a target affected by the spell. Add the number rolled as a bonus to the hit points regained or the damage dealt. The Bardic Inspiration die is then lost. ^766e4a

## Expertise

At 3rd level, choose two of your [[Ability Scores#Skills|skill]] proficiencies. Your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of the chosen proficiencies.

At 10th level, you can choose another two skill proficiencies to gain this benefit.

## Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one [[Ability Scores|ability score]] of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Bardic Versatility (Optional)

Whenever you reach a level in this class that grants the [[Bard#Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feature, you can do one of the following, representing a change in focus as you use your skills and magic: ^9fd28f

- Replace one of the [[Ability Scores#Skills|skills]] you chose for the [[Bard#Expertise|Expertise feature]] with one of your other skill [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiencies]] that isn't benefiting from Expertise.
  ^f2ab28
- Replace one [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrip]] you learned from this class's [[Bard#Spellcasting|Spellcasting feature]] with another cantrip from the [[Bard Spell List]]. ^c805b3

## Font of Inspiration

Beginning when you reach 5th level, you regain all of your expended uses of [[Bard#Bardic Inspiration|Bardic Inspiration]] when you finish a [[Resting Rules#Short Rest|short]] or [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long]] rest.

## Countercharm

At 6th level, you gain the ability to use musical notes or words of power to disrupt mind-influencing effects. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can start a performance that lasts until the end of your next turn. During that time, you and any friendly creatures within 30 feet of you have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] against being [[Conditions#Frightened|frightened]] or [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]]. A creature must be able to hear you to gain this benefit. The performance ends early if you are [[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or silenced or if you voluntarily end it (no action required).

## Magical Secrets

By 10th level, you have plundered magical knowledge from a wide spectrum of disciplines. Choose two spells from any classes, including this one. A spell you choose must be of a level you can cast, as shown on the [[Bard#Bard Progression Table|Bard table]], or a [[Spellcasting Rules#Cantrips|cantrip]].

The chosen spells count as bard spells for you and are included in the number in the Spells Known column of the Bard table.

You learn two additional spells from any classes at 14th level and again at 18th level.

## Superior Inspiration

At 20th level, when you roll [[Combat Rules#Initiative|initiative]] and have no uses of [[Bard#Bardic Inspiration|Bardic Inspiration]] left, you regain one use.
