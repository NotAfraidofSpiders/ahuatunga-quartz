### Shadow Arts

Starting when you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you can use your ki to duplicate the effects of certain spells. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can spend 2 [[Monk#Ki|ki points]] to cast _[[Spells#Darkness|darkness]]_, _[[Spells#Darkvision|darkvision]]_ , _[[Spells#Pass Without Trace|pass without trace]]_, or _[[Spells#Silence|silence]]_, without providing [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|material components]]. Additionally, you gain the _[[Spells#Minor Illusion|minor illusion]]_ cantrip if you don't already know it. ^b09d73

### Shadow Step

At 6th level, you gain the ability to step from one shadow into another. When you are in [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]], as a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]] you can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]]. You then have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on the first [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee attack]] you make before the end of the turn.

### Cloak of Shadows

By 11th level, you have learned to become one with the shadows. When you are in an area of [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]], you can use your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to become [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]]. You remain invisible until you make an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]], [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast a spell]], or are in an area of [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]].

### Opportunist

At 17th level, you can exploit a creature's momentary distraction when it is hit by an attack. Whenever a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack made by a creature other than you, you can use your [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reaction]] to make a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee attack]] against that creature.
