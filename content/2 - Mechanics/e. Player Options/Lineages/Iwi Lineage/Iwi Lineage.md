# The Iwi

[[1 - Island of Ahuatunga]] is home to the Iwi, a rich cast of people connected to any one of the six elements: Fire, Air, Water, Ice, Lightning, or Earth.  Each element has a home region on the island and a distinct culture. And while the tribes of the island are friendly to each other, tension is brewing. Some iwi have a very weak affinity to their element, or no connection at all; these iwi are sometimes referred to as [[Takoto Kau]]. Iwi age normally until they reach maturity, then they seem to stop aging. Once an iwi comes of age, they begin to wear [[Hipoki]], and continue wearing them for the rest of their lives.

Iwi can have children, but across most of the island it’s frowned upon, without another iwi dying first. The island was a gift to the Iwi, and fear of overpopulation keeps most iwi from having children.

# Iwi  Traits

## Ability Score Increase

You increase one ability score of your choice by 2, and a different score of your choice by 1.

## Age

Iwi reach the age of maturity between 18 and 21 years old, but do not seem to have a maximum lifespan.

## Size

Adult iwi span a size range between 4.5 and 7 ft. tall. Your size is medium.

## Speed

Your walking speed is 30 ft.
