_Attuned to the element of fire, [[Iwi Lineage|Ahiwi]] usually make their homes in the fires and magma flows of [[Mangai - The Blaze Eternal|Mangai]]. They are typically hot headed and fierce warriors; using cunning tactics and impeccable technique alongside their fiery disposition to best their foes._

## Fire Resistance

You have [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to Fire Damage.

## Blazing Blood

At certain levels you learn elemental spells. You can cast these spells without verbal or material components.

- At 1st level you learn either the [[Spells#Control Flames|Control Flames]] or [[Spells#Produce Flame|Produce Flame]] cantrip (your choice).
- At 3rd Level you learn either the [[Spells#Burning Hands|Burning Hands]] or [[Spells#Hellish Rebuke|Hellish Rebuke]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
- At 5th Level you learn either the [[Spells#Flame Blade|Flame Blade]] or [[Spells#Aganazzar's Scorcher|Aganazzar's Scorcher]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
