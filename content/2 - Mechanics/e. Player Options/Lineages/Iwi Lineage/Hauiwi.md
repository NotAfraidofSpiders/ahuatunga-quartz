_Connected to the element of air, [[Iwi Lineage|Hauiwi]] claim the floating islands of [[Kauae - Elemental Homeland of Air|Kauae]] as their home on Ahuatunga. Hauiwi are usually free spirited and chaotic, but friendly. They have many friends on the island and are excellent improvisers. (Much to the chagrin of the strategists and tacticians of the island!) Hauiwi are more connected to nature and plant life than other iwi, likely due to their jungle home._

## Fall Resistance

You have resistance to [[Environment Rules#Falling|fall damage]].

## Feather-light Soul

At certain levels you learn elemental spells. You can cast these spells without [[Spellcasting Rules#Verbal (V)|verbal]] or [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|material]] components. ^3e8c22

- At 1st Level you learn either the [[Spells#Gust|Gust]] or [[Spells#Thorn Whip|Thorn Whip]] (your choice) cantrip.     ^ed1f26
- At 3rd Level you learn either the [[Spells#Fog Cloud|Fog Cloud]] or [[Spells#Jump|Jump]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. ^67e89b
- At 5th Level you learn either the [[Spells#Wind Wall|Wind Wall]] or [[Spells#Warding Wind|Warding Wind]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
