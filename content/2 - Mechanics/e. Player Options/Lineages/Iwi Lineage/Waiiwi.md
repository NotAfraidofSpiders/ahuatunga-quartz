_[[Iwi Lineage|Waiiwi]] live in harmony with the element of water, primarily inhabiting [[Naho - Domain of Water]]. They live on giant lily pads and underwater within The Sunken City. They are adept swimmers and usually the most religious of all the iwi, using the healing and rejuvenating properties of water in tandem with its physical power. They are wise and level headed, usually preferring diplomacy to violence, but can be fierce foes when necessary._

## Natural Swimmers

You have a 30 foot [[Monster Rules#Swim|swim speed]] and can [[Environment Rules#Suffocating|hold your breath]] 10 times longer than normal.

## One with the Tide

At certain levels you learn elemental spells. You can cast these spells without verbal or material components.

- At 1st Level you learn the [[Spells#Shape Water|Shape Water]] cantrip.
- At 3rd Level you learn either the [[Spells#Healing Elixir (UA)|Healing Elixir]] or [[Spells#Create or Destroy Water|Create or Destroy Water]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
- At 5th Level you learn either the [[Spells#Wall of Water|Wall of Water]] or [[Spells#Water Walk|Water Walk]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
