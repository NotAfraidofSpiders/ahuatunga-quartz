_Some [[Iwi Lineage|iwi]] lack any sort of elemental gift. These iwi are sometimes referred to as “Takoto Kau.” The term is often used as an insult, though iwi who lack elemental prowess most often make up for it with determination and skill._

## A Diverse Island

While it is encouraged for you to use the one of the lineages above, if you would really like to use a lineage from another source, replace the Age and Size of that lineage with the iwi age and size, and use the remaining traits of the lineage you chose.
