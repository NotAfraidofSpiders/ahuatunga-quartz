_Connected to the element of earth, [[Iwi Lineage|whenuiwi]] claim the caverns of [[Tiro - Earthen Foundation|Tiro]] as their home. They are generally very educated, seeking to fully understand the world around them, to better understand themselves. While their temper is hard to invoke, their wrath is like an earthquake once unleashed._

## Tunnel Dwellers

You have [[Environment Rules#Darkvision|darkvision]] to a range of 60 feet. Additionally, you gain one of the following benefits:

- You gain a 15 ft. [[Monster Rules#Burrow|burrow]] speed which leaves a tunnel behind you.  ^fcc5f6
- You ignore [[Movement Rules#Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] made of earth or stone and you can use an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] while wielding a tool such as a pickax to clear a path large enough for a [[Combat Rules#Size Category Table|medium creature]] through up to 5 ft. of solid stone.

## Terran Vigil

At certain levels you learn elemental spells. You can cast these spells without verbal or material components. ^baa6ee

- At 1st Level you learn either the [[Spells#Magic Stone|Magic Stone]] or [[Spells#Mold Earth|Mold Earth]] cantrip (your choice). ^5569fa
- At 3rd Level you learn either the [[Spells#Earth Tremor|Earth Tremor]] or [[Spells#Earthbind|Earthbind]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. ^f0e513
- At 5th Level you learn either the [[Spells#Wall of Sand|Wall of Sand]] or [[Spells#Erupting Earth|Erupting Earth]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
