_Stoic and self-sufficient, one might describe the typical [[Iwi Lineage|tioiwi]] as icy. This is fitting given their affinity for the element of ice. They call the icy peaks of the [[Ihu - First Snowhome]] their home, and are usually thoughtful and philosophical; taking the time to understand a situation before acting, and to contemplate the stars and wall of prophecy so as to make the best decision._

## Cold Resistance

You have [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to cold damage.

## Frigid Heart

At certain levels you learn elemental spells. You can cast these spells without verbal or material components. ^bfa691

- At 1st Level you learn either the [[Spells#Frostbite|Frostbite]] or [[Spells#Ray of Frost|Ray of Frost]] cantrip (your choice). ^d8a79b
- At 3rd Level you learn either the [[Spells#Armor of Agathys|Armor of Agathys]] or [[Spells#Ice Knife|Ice Knife]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. ^d04380
- At 5th Level you learn either the [[Spells#Rime's Binding Ice|Rime's Binding Ice]] or [[Spells#Sleet Storm|Sleet Storm]] spell (your choice) and can cast it once per long rest without expending a spell slot. You can also cast that spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
