### Ability Score Increase.

- Your [[Ability Scores#Strength|Strength]] score increases by 2, and your [[Ability Scores#Constitution|Constitution]] score increases by 1.

_**Optional Alternative.**_

- You may instead choose to increase any ability score by 2 and any other ability score by 1.

### Size.

Half-orcs are somewhat larger and bulkier than humans, and they range from 5 to well over 6 feet tall. Your size is [[Combat Rules#Size Category Table|Medium]].

### Speed.

Your base walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] is 30 feet.

### Darkvision.

Thanks to your orc blood, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You have [[Environment Rules#Darkvision|darkvision]] out to a range of 60 ft.

### Menacing.

You gain [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency]] in the [[Ability Scores#Charisma Checks|Intimidation]] skill.

### Relentless Endurance.

When you are [[Combat Rules#Dropping to 0 Hit Points|reduced to 0 hit points]] but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. You can't use this feature again until you finish a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]].

### Savage Attacks.

When you score a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] with a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee weapon attack]], you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.
