# Feats

## Spell Sniper

_Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell_

You have learned techniques to enhance your attacks with certain kinds of spells, gaining the following benefits:

- When you [[Spellcasting Rules#Casting a Spell|cast a spell]] that requires you to make an [[Spellcasting Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]], the spell's range is doubled.
- Your ranged spell attacks ignore [[Combat Rules#Cover|half cover and three-quarters cover]].
- You learn one cantrip that requires an attack roll. Choose the cantrip from the [[Bard Spell List|bard]], [[Cleric Spell List|cleric]], [[Druid Spell List|druid]], [[Sorcerer Spell List|sorcerer]], [[Warlock Spell List|warlock]], or [[Wizard Spell List|wizard]] spell list. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip depends on the spell list you chose from: Charisma for bard, sorcerer, and warlock; Wisdom for cleric or druid; or Intelligence for wizard.

## Telekinetic

You learn to move things with your mind, granting you the following benefits:

- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

- You learn the _[[Spells#Mage Hand|mage hand]]_ cantrip. You can cast it without [[Spellcasting Rules#Components|verbal or somatic components]], and you can make the spectral hand invisible. If you already know this spell, its range increases by 30 feet when you cast it. Its spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

- As a [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus action]], you can try to telekinetically shove one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. When you do so, the target must succeed on a Strength [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat) or be moved 5 feet toward or away from you. A creature can willingly fail this save.
