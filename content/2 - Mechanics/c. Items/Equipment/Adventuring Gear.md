# Adventuring Gear

## Acid

As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can splash the contents of this [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|vial]] onto a creature within 5 feet of you or throw the vial up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. In either case, make a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] against a creature or object, treating the acid as an [[Weapon Rules#Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]]. On a hit, the target takes `dice: 2d6` (2d6) acid damage.

## Alchemist's Fire

This sticky, adhesive fluid ignites when exposed to air. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can throw this flask up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. Make a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] against a creature or object, treating the alchemist's fire as an [[Weapon Rules#Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]]. On a hit, the target takes `dice: 1d4`(1d4) fire damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end this damage by using its action to make a DC 10 [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity check]] to extinguish the flames.

## Antitoxin

A creature that drinks this [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|vial]] of liquid gains [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] against [[Poisons|poison]] for 1 hour. It confers no benefit to [[Monster Rules#Type|undead or constructs]].

## Arcane Focus

An arcane focus is a special item--an orb, a crystal, a rod, a specially constructed staff, a wand-like length of wood, or some similar item--designed to channel the power of arcane spells. A [[Sorcerer|sorcerer]], [[Warlock]], or [[Wizard]] can use such an item as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|spellcasting focus]].

## Ball Bearings

As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can spill these tiny metal balls from their [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|pouch]] to cover a level, square area that is 10 feet on a side. A creature [[Combat Rules#Movement and Position|moving]] across the covered area must succeed on a DC 10 [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or fall [[Conditions#Prone|prone]]. A creature moving through the area at half [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] doesn't need to make the save.

## Block and Tackle

A set of pulleys with a cable threaded through them and a hook to attach to objects, a block and tackle allows you to hoist up to four times [[Ability Scores#Lifting and Carrying|the weight you can normally lift]].

## Book

A book might contain poetry, historical accounts, information pertaining to a particular field of lore, diagrams and notes on gnomish contraptions, or just about anything else that can be represented using text or pictures. A book of spells is a [[Adventuring Gear#Spellbook|spellbook]].

## Caltrops

As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can spread a bag of caltrops to cover a square area that is 5 feet on a side. Any creature that enters the area must succeed on a DC 15 [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or [[Combat Rules#Movement and Position|stop moving this turn]] and take 1 piercing damage. Taking this damage reduces the creature's walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] by 10 feet until the creature regains at least 1 hit point. A creature moving through the area at half speed doesn't need to make the save.

## Candle

For 1 hour, a candle sheds [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 5-foot radius and [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] for an additional 5 feet.

## Case, Crossbow Bolt

This wooden case can hold up to twenty [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|crossbow bolts]].

## Case, Map or Scroll

This cylindrical leather case can hold up to ten rolled-up sheets of paper or five rolled-up sheets of parchment.

## Chain

A chain has 10 hit points. It can be burst with a successful DC 20 [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength check]].

## Climber's Kit

A climber's kit includes special pitons, boot tips, gloves, and a harness. You can use the climber's kit as an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to anchor yourself; when you do, you can't [[Environment Rules#Falling|fall]] more than 25 feet from the point where you anchored yourself, and you can't [[Movement Rules#Climbing, Swimming, and Crawling|climb]] more than 25 feet away from that point without undoing the anchor.

## Component Pouch

A component pouch is a small, watertight leather belt pouch that has compartments to hold all the [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|material components]] and other special items you need to cast your spells, except for those components that have a specific cost (as indicated in a spell's description).

## Crowbar

Using a crowbar grants advantage to [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength checks]] where the crowbar's leverage can be applied.

## Druidic Focus

A druidic focus might be a sprig of mistletoe or holly, a wand or scepter made of yew or another special wood, a staff drawn whole out of a living tree, or a totem object incorporating feathers, fur, bones, and teeth from sacred animals. A druid can use such an object as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|spellcasting focus]].

## Fishing Tackle

This kit includes a wooden rod, silken line, corkwood bobbers, steel hooks, lead sinkers, velvet lures, and narrow netting.

## Healer's Kit

This kit is a leather [[Adventuring Gear#Pouch|pouch]] containing bandages, salves, and splints. The kit has ten uses. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can expend one use of the kit to [[Combat Rules#Stabilizing a Creature|stabilize]] a creature that has 0 hit points, without needing to make a [[Ability Scores#Wisdom|Wisdom (Medicine) check]].

## Holy Symbol

A holy symbol is a representation of a god or pantheon. It might be an amulet depicting a symbol representing a deity, the same symbol carefully engraved or inlaid as an emblem on a [[Armor#Shield|shield]], or a tiny box holding a fragment of a sacred relic. A [[Cleric]] or [[Paladin]] can use a holy symbol as a [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|spellcasting focus]]. To use the symbol in this way, the caster must hold it in hand, wear it visibly, or bear it on a shield.

## Holy Water

As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can splash the contents of this [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|flask]] onto a creature within 5 feet of you or throw it up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. In either case, make a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] against a target creature, treating the holy water as an [[Weapon Rules#Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]]. If the target is a [[Monster Rules#Type|fiend or undead]], it takes `dice: 2d6` (2d6) radiant damage.

A [[Cleric]] or [[Paladin]] may create holy water by performing a special ritual. The ritual takes 1 hour to perform, uses 25 gp worth of powdered silver, and requires the caster to expend a 1st-level [[Spellcasting Rules#Spell Slots|spell slot]].

## Hunting Trap

When you use your [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]] to set it, this trap forms a saw-toothed steel ring that snaps shut when a creature steps on a pressure plate in the center. The trap is affixed by a heavy [[Adventuring Gear#Chain|chain]] to an immobile object, such as a tree or a spike driven into the ground. A creature that steps on the plate must succeed on a DC 13 [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Dexterity saving throw]] or take `dice: 1d4` (1d4) piercing damage and stop moving. Thereafter, until the creature breaks free of the trap, its [[Combat Rules#Movement and Position|movement]] is limited by the length of the chain (typically 3 feet long). A creature can use its action to make a DC 13 [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength check]], freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success. Each failed check deals 1 piercing damage to the trapped creature.

## Lamp

A lamp casts [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 15-foot radius and [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] for an additional 30 feet. Once lit, it burns for 6 hours on a [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|flask]] (1 pint) of [[Adventuring Gear#Oil|oil]].

## Lantern, Bullseye

A bullseye lantern casts [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 60-foot [[Spellcasting Rules#Cone|cone]] and [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] for an additional 60 feet. Once lit, it burns for 6 hours on a [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|flask]] (1 pint) of [[Adventuring Gear#Oil|oil]].

## Lantern, Hooded

A hooded lantern casts [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] for an additional 30 feet. Once lit, it burns for 6 hours on a [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|flask]] (1 pint) of [[Adventuring Gear#Oil|oil]]. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can lower the hood, reducing the light to dim light in a 5-foot radius.

## Lock

A key is provided with the lock. Without the key, a creature proficient with [[Tools#Thieves’ Tools|thieve's tools]] can pick this lock with a successful DC 15 [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity check]]. Your DM may decide that better locks are available for higher prices.

## Magnifying Glass

This lens allows a closer look at small objects. It is also useful as a substitute for flint and steel when starting fires. Lighting a fire with a magnifying glass requires light as bright as sunlight to focus, tinder to ignite, and about 5 minutes for the fire to ignite. A magnifying glass grants [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on any [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] made to appraise or inspect an item that is small or highly detailed.

## Manacles

These metal restraints can bind a [[Combat Rules#Creature Size|Small or Medium]] creature. Escaping the manacles requires a successful DC 20 [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity check]]. Breaking them requires a successful DC 20 [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength check]]. Each set of manacles comes with one key. Without the key, a creature proficient with [[Tools#Thieves’ Tools|thieves' tools]] can pick the manacles' lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity check. Manacles have 15 hit points.

## Mess Kit

This tin box contains a cup and simple cutlery. The box clamps together, and one side can be used as a cooking pan and the other as a plate or shallow bowl.

## Oil

Oil usually comes in a clay [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|flask]] that holds 1 pint. As an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]], you can splash the oil in this flask onto a creature within 5 feet of you or throw it up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. Make a [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged attack]] against a target creature or object, treating the oil as an [[Weapon Rules#Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]]. On a hit, the target is covered in oil. If the target takes any fire damage before the oil dries (after 1 minute), the target takes an additional 5 fire damage from the burning oil. You can also pour a flask of oil on the ground to cover a 5-foot-square area, provided that the surface is level. If lit, the oil burns for 2 rounds and deals 5 fire damage to any creature that enters the area or ends its turn in the area. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.

## Poison, Basic

You can use the [[Poisons|poison]] in this [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|vial]] to coat one [[Weapon Rules#Weapons Table|slashing or piercing weapon]] or up to three pieces of [[Adventuring Gear#Ammunition Table|ammunition]]. Applying the poison takes an action. A creature hit by the poisoned weapon or ammunition must make a DC 10 [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Constitution saving throw]] or take `dice: 1d4` (1d4) poison damage. Once applied, the poison retains potency for 1 minute before drying.

## Pouch

A cloth or leather pouch [[Adventuring Gear#Container Capacity|can hold up to]] 20 sling bullets or 50 blowgun needles, among other things. A compartmentalized pouch for holding [[Spellcasting Rules#Material (M)|spell components]] is called a [[Adventuring Gear#Component Pouch.|component pouch]].

## Quiver

A quiver can hold up to 20 arrows.

## Ram, Portable

You can use a portable ram to break down doors. When doing so, you gain a +4 bonus on the [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength check]]. One other character can [[Combat Rules#Help|help]] you use the ram, giving you [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on this check.

## Rations

Rations consist of dry [[Environment Rules#Food|foods]] suitable for extended travel, including jerky, dried fruit, hardtack, and nuts.

## Rope

Rope, whether made of hemp or silk, has 2 hit points and can be burst with a DC 17 [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength check]].

## Scale, Merchant's

A scale includes a small balance, pans, and a suitable assortment of weights up to 2 pounds. With it, you can measure the exact weight of small objects, such as raw precious metals or trade goods, to help [[Wealth Rules#Selling Treasure|determine their worth]].

## Spellbook

Essential for [[Wizard|wizards]], a spellbook is a [[Adventuring Gear#Book|leather-bound tome]] with 100 blank vellum pages suitable for recording spells.

## Spyglass

Objects viewed through a spyglass are magnified to twice their size.

## Tent

A simple and portable canvas shelter, a tent sleeps two.

## Tinderbox

This small container holds flint, fire steel, and tinder (usually dry cloth soaked in light oil) used to kindle a fire. Using it to light a torch--or anything else with abundant, exposed fuel--takes an [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|action]]. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.

## Torch

A torch burns for 1 hour, providing [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] for an additional 20 feet. If you make a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee attack]] with a burning torch and hit, it deals 1 fire damage.
