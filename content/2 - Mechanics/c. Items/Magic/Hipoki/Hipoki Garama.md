The mask of growth, the [[Hipoki Overview|Hipoki]] Garama allows its user to wield physical size as weapon.

## Passive

While wearing the Hipoki Garama, it's wearer counts as one [[Combat Rules#Creature Size|size]] larger for the purposes of determining it's [[Ability Scores#Lifting and Carrying|carrying capacity]] and the maximum size of a creature you can [[Combat Rules#Grappling|grapple]].

## Noble

As an action, you and [[Oversized Weapons|your equipment]] can increase in [[Combat Rules#Creature Size|size, up to large]], as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.

## Great

As a bonus action, you and [[Oversized Weapons|your equipment]] can increase in size up to huge as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.
