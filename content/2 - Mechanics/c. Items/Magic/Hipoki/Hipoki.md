# Hipoki

## Masks of Power

Hipoki are to Iwi as clothes are to humans. Magical by nature, a Hipoki is worn by all iwi who are of age, lest they fall into a cursed sleep akin to the slumber of their creator. There are three varieties of hipoki: Mundane, Noble, and Great. Mundane hipoki all grant some benefit based on the shape and material used in the construction of the mask. Noble hipoki have useful abilities which can be used through concentration; A noble hipoki cannot be crafted, rather, time or deed transforms a hipoki into a noble hipoki. Great hipoki are the rarest of the hipoki, and while they can be crafted, they require special materials and rituals to make. The most famous great hipoki are the masks of the Kaitiaki, who were made into great hipoki by the Primeval Spirits when they took up the mantle as defenders of the Iwi.

Hipoki all work in a similar way: Every hipoki has a small innate effect while worn. Most are niche but they all serve a purpose. Great and noble hipoki also have active effects that can be concentrated on to use. Great hipoki are more powerful than their noble counterparts, but much rarer to come across.

## Types of Hipoki

### Great Hipoki

Great hipoki are worn by the [[Kaitiaki]] and other similar heroes of great power. They are incredibly difficult to make, but it can be done be mask makers of great expertise. They are formidable relics on their own; the fact that they are only worn by creatures powerful enough to acquire them without a great hipoki only makes them more coveted and rare.

### Noble Hipoki

Noble hipoki cannot be crafted, unlike their mundane and great counterparts. They instead transform from either a great or mundane variant when its wearer commits a great act of selflessness. They are less powerful than great hipoki, but their wearers command great respect due to the nature of noble hipoki.

## Forgoing a Hipoki

For an adult iwi, not wearing a hipoki is risky business. Any iwi that forgoes wearing a hipoki, or has theirs removed receives the following penalties:

- When not wearing a hipoki you have a -2 to all attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws
- After each hour spent without a hipoki you gain a level of exhaustion.
  - The sixth level of exhaustion puts you into a coma, instead of killing you outright.
    - These levels of exhaustion can only be removed while wearing a hipoki.

## Making a Hipoki

Mask making is a very difficult process that few know and even fewer master.

### Mask Material

Mask making is a noble art that very few are capable of. The ancient craft of fabricating a mask to express the iwi who bears it is as mysterious as it is difficult.  Hipoki are magical masks, usually made of carved wood or stone, but rarely made from metals. They are adorned with shells, plants, gems, claws, horns, and fur; each material with its own significance and magical effect. There is a mask maker currently on the island, though their identity is not known.

Mask making can be dangerous too. The act of concealing one’s soul has dire consequences if not done properly. If a mask maker creates a mask that hides too much it can claim a great cost. Iwi have been known to become twisted versions of themselves or worse: become horrible monstrosities all together.
