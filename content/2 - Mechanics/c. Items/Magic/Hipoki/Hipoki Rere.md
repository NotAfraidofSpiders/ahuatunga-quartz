The mask of levitation. The [[Hipoki Overview]] Rere allows its user to make themselves as light as a breeze.

## Passive

You gain [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on any [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] or [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throw]] required to [[Movement Rules#Climbing, Swimming, and Crawling|climb]] or land safely after a [[Environment Rules#Falling|fall]].

## Noble

Instead of providing the user with true levitation, it instead allows the user to slow any fall to a survivable rate. As a reaction in response to falling while wearing the mask, you may reduce the speed of your fall to 60 feet per round while you [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentrate]], as if concentrating on a spell. While falling at this speed you take no [[Environment Rules#Falling|fall damage]].

## Great

While wearing the mask, you can use your bonus action to become weightless as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell. While levitating in this way you can move vertically up or down up to 20 feet as part of your movement. Otherwise you can only move by pushing or pulling yourself along a fixed object or surface - which allows you to move as if you were climbing - or by being pushed by a strong wind such as those created by the _[[Spells#Gust|gust]]_ cantrip or _[[Spells#Gust of Wind|gust of wind]]_ spell.
