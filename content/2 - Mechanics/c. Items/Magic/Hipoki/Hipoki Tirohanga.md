The mask of vision, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Tirohanga allows its user to see through illusions and to see minute detail and slight motions.

## Passive

While wearing this mask you gain a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Skills|perception and investigation]] checks relying on sight equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]].

## Noble

The noble Tirohanga allows a much more limited scope than its great counterpart. As an action while wearing the mask you gain [[Environment Rules#Truesight|truesight]] to a distance of 10 feet, so long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.

## Great

While wearing this mask you can use your action to gain [[Environment Rules#Truesight|truesight]] to a distance of 30 feet, so long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell. While concentrating in this way, you may ignore [[Environment Rules#Lightly Obscured|light obscurement]] within the range of your truesight and treat [[Environment Rules#Heavily Obscured|heavily obscured areas]] within this range as if they were lightly obscured.
