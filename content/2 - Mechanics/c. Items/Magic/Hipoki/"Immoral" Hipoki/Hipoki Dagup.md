The [[Hipoki Overview]] Dagup, the mask of incomprehension, allows its wearer to scramble and obfuscate language in order to confuse or deceive.

## Passive

While wearing this hipoki, you gain a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Skills|deception]] checks equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]].

## Noble

As a bonus action while wearing this hipoki, you can begin to scramble any written text you can touch, up to one page. At the start of your next turn, if you maintained [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell, it becomes incomprehensible gibberish. Anyone wearing a [[Hipoki Rau]] can still read the scrambled text normally.

## Great

As a bonus action, you can cause either of the following effects:

- Up to one page of writing you can see within 60ft of you begins to become unintelligible. At the start of your next turn, if you maintained [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell, it becomes incomprehensible gibberish. Anyone wearing a [[Hipoki Rau]] can still read the scrambled text normally.
- One creature you can see within 30ft. of you cannot speak or write except in gibberish for as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell. While afflicted in this way, a creature cannot cast spells that require [[Spellcasting Rules#Verbal (V)|verbal components]].
