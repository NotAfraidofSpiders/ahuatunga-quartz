The mask of water breathing, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Manawa allows its wearer to stay underwater for longer and more gracefully while submerged.

## Passive

While wearing the Hipoki Manawa you can [[Environment Rules#Holding your Breath|hold your breath]] twice as long as normal.

## Noble

While wearing the mask you can use your bonus action to gain the ability to breathe underwater as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.

## Great

While wearing the mask, you can use your bonus action to allow yourself to breath underwater as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]]; additionally, while concentrating in this way, attacks you make while underwater [[Combat Rules#Underwater Combat|do not have disadvantage]] if you do not have a [[Monster Rules#Swim|swim speed]], and ranged attacks [[Combat Rules#Underwater Combat|no longer automatically miss when made at long range underwater]].
