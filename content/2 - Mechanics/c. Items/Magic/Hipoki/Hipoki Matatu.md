The mask of telekinesis, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Matatu allows its wearer the ability to move objects with their mind. Craftsmen and merchants of the island often employ a Matatu to assist in their workshops and stores, using it to retrieve tools and goods from across a room.

## Passive

While wearing this mask you can use telekinesis to move and manipulate the world around you. You can move one [[Object Rules|object]] object not being worn or carried that you can see within 30 ft. of you as if you were moving it yourself. For example you can manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial. Any object you move cannot exceed 10 pounds.

## Noble

As an action, you can manipulate one or more [[Object Rules|objects]] you can see, that are not [[Environment Rules#Lightly Obscured|lightly]] or [[Environment Rules#Heavily Obscured|heavily]] obscured, and weigh no more than 75 pounds in total. If an object is being carried by another creature, make an [[Ability Scores#Intelligence Checks|Intelligence check]] [[Ability Scores#Contests|contested]] by that creature’s [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength (Athletics) check]]. If you succeed the creature drops what it was holding. You can only manipulate objects in this way as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if concentrating on a spell.
