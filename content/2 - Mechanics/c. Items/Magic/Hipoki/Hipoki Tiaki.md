The mask of shielding, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Tiaki protects its wearer from incoming attacks and hazards.

## Passive

Your AC increases by half your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] rounded down (minimum 1) while you wear this mask.

## Noble

You can use a reaction in response to being hit by an [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] to increase your AC by your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]], potentially turning the hit into a miss. You retain this bonus to your AC for as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.

## Great

The great Tiaki greatly increases its wearer's resiliency. As an action while wearing this mask, you may reduce all damage you take by 1d12 + your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] for as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.
