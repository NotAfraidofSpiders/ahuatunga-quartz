The mask of concealment, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Huna grants its wearer the ability to hide themselves in plain sight, and in some cases even turn completely invisible.

## Passive

The magic of the mask conceals you from observation. You are always [[Environment Rules#Lightly Obscured|lightly obscured]] when wearing this mask.

## Noble

The noble Huna allows its wearer to turn [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] as a bonus action. You remain invisible until you [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|move, take an action, bonus action, reaction]], or lose [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if concentrating on a spell.
