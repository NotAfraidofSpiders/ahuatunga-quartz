The mask of seeking, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Ruru allows its wearer to find things they might otherwise miss.

## Passive

While wearing the Ruru, you gain a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Skills|investigation and perception]] checks made to find [[Ability Scores#Hiding|concealed creatures]] and hidden structures such as [[Trap Rules|traps]] and secret doors equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]].

## Noble

As a bonus action, you cause yourself to perceive [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] as [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]], and [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]] as [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]] as if you had [[Environment Rules#Darkvision|darkvision]], however you can still discern colors in darkness. You continue to see in this way as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.
