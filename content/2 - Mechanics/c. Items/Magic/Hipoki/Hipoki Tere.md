The [[Hipoki Overview|Hipokit]] Tere, otherwise known as the mask of speed, allows its user to move incredibly quickly, in some cases turning its user into little more than a blur passing by.

## Passive

While you wear this mask your walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] increases by 5 ft.

## Noble

While wearing a noble Tere, you can use a bonus action to increase your walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] by an additional 5 feet so long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.

## Great

While wearing this mask, you can use an action to increase your walking [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] by an additional 5 feet, and grant yourself the ability to [[Combat Rules#Dash|dash]] as a bonus action so long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell.
