The mask of strength, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Kaha allows its user to accomplish incredible feats of strength.

## Passive:

While wearing a Hipoki Kaha, your [[Ability Scores#Lifting and Carrying|carrying capacity]] is doubled and you add a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Strength Checks|Strength (Athletics) checks]] equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]].

## Noble:

While still allowing for peak physical performance, the noble Kaha doesn't allow its user to perform superhuman feats like its great cousin. While wearing a noble Kaha, you can use a bonus action to gain [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability checks]] and [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] relying on strength.

## Great:

While wearing a great Kaha, you can use your action to gain a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability checks]], [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]] and [[Combat Rules#Damage Rolls|damage rolls]] using strength equal to your [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell. Additionally, while concentrating in this way your [[Ability Scores#Lifting and Carrying|carrying capacity]] is doubled again (4x what it normally would be).
