While called the mask of mind control, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Komau is a strange mask. It doesn’t allow for true mind control, just the ability to influence other creatures' moods and behaviors.

## Passive

While wearing this mask you gain an intuitive sense of the emotions of people around you. You gain a bonus to [[Ability Scores#Skills|Insight and Persuasion]] checks equal to your proficiency bonus while wearing the Komau. 

## Noble

As an action while wearing a noble Komau, you can convince a [[Monster Rules#type|beast or monstrosity]] that you can see within 30 ft. of you that you mean it no harm. It must succeed on a DC 15 [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|Wisdom saving throw]], or be [[Conditions#Charmed|charmed]] charmed by you as long as you maintain [[Spellcasting Rules#Concentration|concentration]], as if on a spell, or until you or your companions harm the target. While charmed in this way, you can telepathically communicate with it, and it can communicate simple emotions and concepts back to you.
