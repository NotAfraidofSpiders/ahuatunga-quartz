The mask of translation, the [[Hipoki Overview]] Rau, is often used by scholars in the archives to decipher the mysterious glyphs on Ahuatunga.

## Passive

This mask allows you to read all written languages while wearing it, though it grants you no special ability to understand ciphers or hidden codes.

## Noble

While wearing a noble Rau you can use your bonus action to gain the ability to comprehend and verbally communicate with [[Monster Rules#Type|beasts]] for the duration. The knowledge and awareness of many beasts is limited by their intelligence, but at minimum, beasts can give you information about nearby locations and monsters, including whatever they can perceive or have perceived within the past day. You might be able to persuade a beast to perform a small favor for you, at the DM’s discretion.
