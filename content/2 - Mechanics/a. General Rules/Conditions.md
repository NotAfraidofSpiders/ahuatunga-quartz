# Published Conditions

## Blinded

- A blinded creature can't see and automatically fails any [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] that requires sight.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]], and the creature's attack rolls have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].

## Charmed

- A charmed creature can't attack the charmer or target the charmer with harmful abilities or magical effects.
- The charmer has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] on any [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] to interact socially with the creature.

## Deafened

- A deafened creature can't hear and automatically fails [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability check]] check that requires hearing.

## Frightened

- A frightened creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks|ability checks]] and [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] while the source of its fear is within line of sight.
- The creature can't willingly move closer to the source of its fear.

## Grappled

- A [[Combat Rules#Grappling|grappled creature's]] speed becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed.
- The condition ends if the grappler is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]].
- The condition also ends if an effect removes the grappled creature from the reach of the grappler or grappling effect, such as when a creature is hurled away by the [[Spells#Thunderwave|Thunderwave]] spell.

## Incapacitated

- An incapacitated creature can't take [[Combat Rules#Your Turn|actions]] or [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reactions]]

## Invisible

- An invisible creature is impossible to see without the aid of magic or a special sense. For the purpose of hiding, the creature is heavily obscured. The creature's location can be detected by any noise it makes or any tracks it leaves.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]], and the creature's attack rolls have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]].

## Paralyzed

- A paralyzed creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]] and can't move or speak.
- The creature automatically fails strength and dexterity saving throws.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]].
- Any attack that hits the creature is a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature.

## Petrified

- A petrified creature is transformed, along with any nonmagical object it is wearing or carrying, into a solid inanimate substance (usually stone). Its weight increases by a factor of ten, and it ceases aging.
- The creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], can't move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]].
- The creature automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- The creature has [[Combat Rules#Damage Resistance and Vulnerability|resistance]] to all damage.
- The creature is immune to [[Poisons|poison]] and [[Diseases|disease]], although a poison or disease already in its system is suspended, not neutralized.

## Poisoned

- A poisoned creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]] and ability checks.

## Prone

- A prone creature's only movement option is to [[Movement Rules#Climbing, Swimming, and Crawling|crawl]], unless it stands up and thereby ends the condition.
- The creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack rolls]].
- An attack roll against the creature has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature. Otherwise, the attack roll has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].

## Restrained

- A restrained creature's [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]], and the creature's attack rolls have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]].
- The creature has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

## Stunned

- A stunned creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], can't move, and can speak only falteringly.
- The creature automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]].

## Unconscious

- An unconscious creature is [[Conditions#Incapacitated|incapacitated]], can't move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings.
- The creature drops whatever it's holding and falls [[Conditions#Prone|prone]].
- [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|Attack rolls]] against the creature have [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|advantage]].
- Any attack that hits the creature is a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of the creature.

## Exhaustion

Some special abilities and environmental hazards, such as starvation and the long-term effects of [[Temperature and Weather Rules|freezing or scorching temperatures]], can lead to a special condition called exhaustion. Exhaustion is measured in six levels. An effect can give a creature one or more levels of exhaustion, as specified in the effect's description.

### Exhaustion effects

| Level | Effect                                                                                                           |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage\|Disadvantage]] on [[Ability Scores#Ability Checks\|ability checks]] |
| 2     | [[Movement Rules#Speed\|Speed]] halved                                                                           |
| 3     | Disadvantage on [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls\|attack rolls]] and [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws\|saving throws]]  |
| 4     | [[Combat Rules#Hit Points\|Hit point maximum]] halved                                                            |
| 5     | Speed reduced to 0                                                                                               |
| 6     | Death                                                                                                            |
If an already exhausted creature suffers another effect that causes exhaustion, its current level of exhaustion increases by the amount specified in the effect's description.

A creature suffers the effect of its current level of exhaustion as well as all lower levels. For example, a creature suffering level 2 exhaustion has its speed halved and has disadvantage on ability checks.

An effect that removes exhaustion reduces its level as specified in the effect's description, with all exhaustion effects ending if a creature's exhaustion level is reduced below 1.

Finishing a [[Resting Rules#Long Rest|long rest]] long rest reduces a creature's exhaustion level by 1, provided that the creature has also ingested some food and drink.

# Homebrew Conditions

**The following content is homebrew written for the Ahuatunga Campaign setting**

## Bleeding

A creature affected by the bleeding condition takes damage equal to its [[Ability Scores#Proficiency Bonus|proficiency bonus]] at the start of each of its turns. This condition ends for an affected creature when it regains hit points or spends a full turn without [[Combat Rules#Movement and Position|moving]].

## Unstable

### Becoming Unstable

When you are [[Combat Rules#Dropping to 0 Hit Points|reduced to zero hit points]], you become unstable.

### While Unstable

At the start of your first turn while unstable roll a d4. You die at the start of your turn after a number of rounds equal to the number rolled. 

While you are unstable your [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] is halved, and you can move only by [[Movement Rules#Climbing, Swimming, and Crawling|crawling]]. Additionally, you can only take the [[Combat Rules#Dodge|dodge]], [[Combat Rules#Disengage|disengage]] and [[Combat Rules#Help|help]] actions, cannot take [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus actions]] or [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reactions]], and can speak only falteringly. 

You automatically fail strength and dexterity [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]]. Any [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] that hits you is automatically a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you. 

If you would fail a [[Combat Rules#Death Saving Throws|death saving throw]], instead reduce the number of rounds until you die by one. For example: if you take damage while unstable, reduce the number of rounds you have left by one, or by two if the damage is from a critical hit. If the number of rounds you have left is ever equal to or less than zero, you die.

### Ending the Condition

While unstable, if you regain hit points or become [[Combat Rules#Stabilizing a Creature|stabilized]] by some other means such as the _[[Spells#Spare the Dying|spare the dying]]_ Cantrip, the condition ends. If you still have 0 hit points when the condition ends you gain the [[Conditions#Downed|Downed Condition]]

## Downed

### Gaining the Downed Condition

When you have zero hit points but are not [[Conditions#Unstable|unstable]] you gain the downed condition.

When you become downed, roll a d4. The condition ends on you after a number of hours equal to 1 + the number rolled, or when you regain hit points.

### While Downed

While you are downed your [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]] is halved, and you can move only by [[Movement Rules#Climbing, Swimming, and Crawling|crawling]]. Additionally, you can only take the [[Combat Rules#Dodge|dodge]], [[Combat Rules#Disengage|disengage]], and [[Combat Rules#Help|help]] actions, cannot take [[Combat Rules#Bonus Actions|bonus actions]] or [[Combat Rules#Reactions|reactions]], and can speak only falteringly. 

You automatically fail strength and dexterity [[Ability Scores#Saving Throws|saving throws]]. Any [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack]] that hits you is automatically a [[Combat Rules#Critical Hits|critical hit]] if the attacker is within five feet of you. 

If you would fail a [[Combat Rules#Death Saving Throws|death saving throw]], instead you [[Conditions#Unstable|become unstable]].
