# A Bang, Not a Whimper

The purposes of these homebrew rules are to make dying more dynamic and narrative impactful in combat. The rules added here aim to make dying less passive, so player retain agency while they are on death's door. It also makes it easier for non-combatant casualties which can allow for new emergent tactics in combat.

## The Unstable Condition

The [[Conditions#Unstable|unstable condition]] is a new condition that [[Combat Rules#Falling Unconscious| falling unconscious when a character reduced to 0 hit points]]. The condition describes what circumstances end it.

## The Downed Condition

The [[Conditions#Downed|downed condition]] functions in much the same way as the unstable condition, except that you aren't at risk of dying.

The condition lasts for 1d4+1 hours or until the downed character regains hit points.

## Desperate Defiance

You force yourself back into the fight for a brief moment. While [[Conditions#Unstable|unstable]] or [[Conditions#Downed|downed]], if you have half or more of your total [[Character Advancement Rules#Hit Points and Hit Dice|hit dice]] remaining, you can expend half of your total hit dice rounded down at the start of your turn to suspend the effects of the unstable, downed, and [[Conditions#Stunned|stunned]] conditions to take a turn as normal.

# Dying

When a character dies, they can cling to life for up to an hour, during which time they are mechanically dead and cannot be healed by any magic or medicine. While in this state, they can maintain enough consciousness to converse quietly with other characters but nothing more.

# The Casualty Rule

If a non-combatant NPC takes damage equal to half of their remaining HP from a single source, instead their hp is reduced to zero.
