These homebrew rules aim to make the choice of weapons more impactful, and to allow combatants who do not use magic to have more versatility

# Damage Type Matters

## Bludgeoning Damage

Whenever a creature deals bludgeoning damage with a weapon attack, the attacker can choose to push the target of the attack away a number of feet equal to 1/3 the damage dealt, rounded down.

## Slashing Damage

Whenever a creature deals slashing damage with a weapon attack, the target of the attack gains the [[Conditions#Bleeding|Bleeding Condition]].

## Piercing Damage

Whenever a creature deals piercing damage on a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, any dice used to determine the piercing damage dealt are treated as having rolled their maximum value. [[Crunchy Crits|This feature does not count as a crunch for the purposes of Crunchy Crits]]

# Unique Weapon Properties

## Daggers

Daggers ignore ½ and ¾ cover penalty on [[Martial Revisions#Called Shots|called shots]] when making a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee attack]], but don't [[Martial Revisions#Piercing Damage|automatically deal maximum damage on a critical hit]].

## Thrown Weapons

[[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|Ranged weapon attacks]] dealing slashing or piercing damage made with weapons that have the [[Weapon Rules#Thrown|thrown property]] stick into their target. Once a turn, when a creature with weapons stuck in it moves using its [[Movement Rules#Speed|speed]], it takes takes 1d4 damage of the type the weapon deals for each thrown weapon stuck in it. A weapon can be removed as an object interaction with a successful DC 10 [[Ability Scores#Skills|medicine check]].

# Flanking

You are flanking a creature when it is within reach of you and one or more of your allies on opposing sides of the creature.

When flanking a creature you gain a +2 attack bonus to [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee weapon attacks]] against the flanked creature.

# Called Shots

Before you make a [[Combat Rules#Melee Attacks|melee]] or [[Combat Rules#Ranged Attacks|ranged]] weapon attack, you can call your shot. Called shots are made at [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]], but do more. [[Armor]], shells, scales, etc. provide cover to the target of a called shot.

##  Example Called Shots

- A successful strike to an opponent’s hand does does no damage but will cause them to drop the weapon or item they’re holding with it. 

- If you strike an opponent’s leg, their dex saves receive a -2 penalty until they bandage the wound. 

- If you hit them in the eye they take no damage but take a -2 to all attack rolls for the rest of the fight. 

- If you strike a vital spot such as the neck or heart on an opponent they take damage as though vulnerable.

# Grappling impedes attacks

A grappled creature also has [[Ability Scores#Advantage and Disadvantage|disadvantage]] on [[Combat Rules#Attack Rolls|attack roll]] against creatures other than the grappler.
