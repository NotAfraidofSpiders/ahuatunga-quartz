# Adding Some Crunch to Critical Hits

This homebrew critical hit revision aims to make getting critical hits more exciting.

Whenever you roll damage for a critical hit, any die which rolls its maximum value explodes.

## Exploding Dice

Exploding is a term used to refer to rolling the die an additional time, adding the new roll to the previous total, and continuing to explode if the conditions are met.
