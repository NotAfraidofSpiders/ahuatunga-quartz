**Source:** Xanathar's Guide to Everything, pg. 78

The rules are purposely left open-ended concerning mundane tasks like tying knots, but sometimes knowing how well a knot was fashioned is important in a dramatic scene when someone is trying to untie a knot or slip out of one. Here's an optional rule for determining the effectiveness of a knot.

The creature who ties the knowt makes an Intellegence (Slight of Hand) check when doing so. The total of the check becomes the DC for an attempt to untie the knot with an Intellegence (Slight of Hand) check or to slip out of it with a [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity (Acrobatics) check]].

This rule intentionally links [[Ability Scores#Skills|Sleight of Hand]] with [[Ability Scores#Intelligence|Intellegence]] rather than [[Ability Scores#Dexterity Checks|Dexterity]]. This is an example of how to apply the rule in the "[[Ability Scores#Variant Skills with Different Abilities|Variant: Skills with Different Abilities]]" section.
