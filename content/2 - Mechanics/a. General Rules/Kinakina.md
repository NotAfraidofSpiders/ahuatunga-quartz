# Synopsis

## Introduction

Kinakina is a game played on a [[Kinakina#Pitch|pitch]] by 3 [[Kinakina#Team|teams]] of 2 players using [[Kinakina#Stick|sticks]] to score goals with a [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]]. Each team has a [[Kinakina#Goal|goal]], and the aim of the game is to have the least number of [[Kinakina#Fault|faults]] at the end of the game. You are assigned a [[Kinakina#Fault|fault]] if the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] enters your team's goal or if you break a [[Kinakina#Official Rules|rule]]. The game ends once any team reaches 12 faults, and the team with the fewest faults wins.

## Playing the Game

The game begins with the puck in the center of the pitch, and no player closer than 10ft away from the puck. Once the horn is blown, players may take any action within the bounds of the rules in order to score on the other teams and secure their victory. After each goal or rules violation, the puck returns to the center of the field, players take their desired spot no closer than 10 feet away from the puck, and play begins again. This cycle repeats until the game ends.

## Ending the Match

There are two ways that a match of Kinakina can end: First, a team is awarded their 12th fault. Second, if any player or the judge dies. If a death occurs during a match for any reason, the game ends immediately, and no team wins.

# Rules

## Definitions

### A Kinakina Pitch is...

A Kinakina pitch is where the game is played. While most pitches are equilateral hexagons, the requirements for a legal pitch are as follows:

- A pitch must have 3 [[Kinakina#Goal|goals]], one for each [[Kinakina#Team|team]].
  - The open face of each goal must be equidistant and equally elevated relative to the geometric center of the pitch.
  - If the position of a goal provides any advantages or disadvantages to its defending team, teams will be randomly assigned their goals before the start of each match.
- A pitch must have exterior walls which clearly define the bounds of play.
- A pitch must be fashioned in such a way that players can observe at least half of the pitch without repositioning.

### A Kinakina Goal is...

A goal is a 15ft x 10ft x 10ft rectangular prism, with a single open face to shoot at.

### A Kinakina Team is...

A Kinakina match is played with 3 teams of no more than 2 players. Teams are determined before play begins, and cannot change during a match.

### A Kinakina Stick is...

A stick for the purposes of Kinakina, is any implement or tool made to assist in the game, approved by the judge before the game. Most sticks have a net for catching and throwing the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] and a hammer for moving the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] very quickly.

The guidelines judges use to determine if a stick is legal or not are as follows:

- A stick should have a linear form.
  - Minimize forking, splitting, or overly wide silhouettes
- A stick should not be able to completely enclose or trap the puck.
  - Opponents should always be able to steal possession of the puck, regardless of the stick used (ease of stealing can vary).

### A Kinakina Puck is...

Kinakina is played with a puck. This puck is not perfectly flat on any face, comprised of a cylindrical body and two convex ends.

- The material of the puck may vary as long as it is a uniform density, size, and weight.
  - The puck is a tiny object that weighs 2.5 pounds.
  - No hollow or multi-material pucks.

### A Kinakina Fault is...

- A fault is a way of marking an error in gameplay. The team that makes the least mistakes, and therefor plays the best, wins the game when it ends. The object of the game, therefor, is not to score the most goals, but to play the best.

## Official Rules

### General Rules

- The game is played on a [[Kinakina#Pitch|pitch]], with 3 [[Kinakina#Team|teams]], a [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]], and one judge.
- Each player is allowed one [[Kinakina#Stick|stick]] during play.
- When a player has possession of the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]], the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] must be in contact with the [[Kinakina#Pitch|pitch]] while that player moves.
- When the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] enters a team's [[Kinakina#Goal|goal]], that team is assigned a [[Kinakina#Fault|fault]].
- The game ends the instant any team has accumulated 12 [[Kinakina#Fault|faults]]. When the game ends in this way, the team with the least number of [[Kinakina#Fault|faults]] wins.
- If a player or judge dies for any reason, the game ends with no winners.
- Failure to comply with any rule assigns the offending player's team a [[Kinakina#Fault|fault]].

### Restrictions

- A player may not restrain a player who does not have possession of the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]].
- Players may not draw blood.
- Players may not unmask a player on another team.
- Players may not destroy the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] or prevent any team for taking possession of the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] for more than 60 seconds per game.
  - All players on the same team use the same 60 second timer for purposes of assigning [[Kinakina#Fault|faults]].
- Players may not assault or otherwise cause harm to another player except for the purpose of gaining possession of the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] or to create the necessary space to take a shot at a [[Kinakina#Goal|goal]] or make a pass to another player.
- Players may not teleport while in possession of the [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]].

## Gaining Possession of the Puck

### Uncontested Pucks

You may gain possession of an uncontested [[Kinakina#Puck|puck]] by using an object interaction to pick it up, or scoop it using your [[Kinakina#Stick|stick]].

### Contested Puck

There are two forms of contested puck: A grounded puck, and an airborne puck.
**Grounded Pucks**

- A puck is grounded when a player has possession of it. To gain possession of a contested puck on the ground, you must win possession from the player who has the puck.

## Handling the Puck

### Bounces

While you are in possession of the puck, the puck's irregular shape causes it to bounce unpredictably when you lose focus on your handling. Whenever you roll a 1 on an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you lose possession of the puck, which bounces 15 feet in a random direction, never moving more than 5 feet from the ground.

## Passing the Puck

### Making a Pass

To make a pass, make an attack against the ally you want to pass to with either a [[Kinakina#Kinakina Puck|Kinakina puck]] or a [[Kinakina#Kinakina Stick|Kinakina stick]].

### Catching a Pass

As a reaction to being the target of a ranged pass, you can make a Dexterity (Athletics) check to attempt to catch the pass. The DC is equal to 25 minus the passer's attack roll (minimum of 1).

### Intercepting a Pass

As a reaction to a creature within 5 feet of you being the target of a pass, you can cause any ability check that creature to roll any ability checks to catch the pass with disadvantage. If the creature does not catch the pass, you win possession of the puck.

### Interfering with a Pass

As a reaction to being within 5 feet of a creature making a pass, you can subtract your proficiency bonus from that creature's attack roll.
