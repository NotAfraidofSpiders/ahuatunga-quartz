# Falling

At the end of a fall, a creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it fell, to a maximum of 20d6. The creature lands [[Conditions#Prone|prone]], unless it avoids taking damage from the fall.

# Suffocating

## Holding your Breath

A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 + its [[Ability Scores#Ability Scores and Modifiers Table|Constitution modifier]] (minimum of 1 round).

## Running out of Air

At the start of its next turn, it drops to 0 hit points and is [[Conditions#Unstable|unstable]], and it can't regain hit points or be [[Conditions#Downed|stabilized]] until it can breath again.

For example, a creature with a Constitution of 14 can hold it's breath for 3 minutes. If it starts suffocating, it has 2 rounds to reach air before it drops to 0 hit points.

# Vision and Light

## Lightly Obscured

- Creatures have disadvantage on [[Ability Scores#Perception|Wisdom (Perception) checks]] that rely on sight.
- Examples:
  - [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|Dim Light]]
  - patchy fog
  - moderate foliage

## Heavily Obscured

- A creature effectively suffers from the [[Conditions#Blinded|blinded condition]] when trying to see something in that area.
- Examples
  - [[Environment Rules#Darkness|Darkness]]
  - Opaque Fog
  - Dense Foliage

## Light

### Bright Light

- Creatures can see normally
- Examples:
  - Daylight
  - Torches
  - Lanterns
  - Fires

### Dim Light

- Creates a [[Environment Rules#Lightly Obscured|lightly obscured]] area.
- Usually the boundary between a source of [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]] and surrounding [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]].
- Examples:
  - Twilight and Dawn
  - Bright Full Moon

### Darkness

- Creates a [[Environment Rules#Heavily Obscured|heavily obscured]] area.
- Examples:
  - Most moonlight
  - Unilluminated areas

## Special Senses

### Blindsight

A creature with blindsight can perceive its surroundings without relying on sight, within a specific radius.

### Darkvision

A creature with darkvision can see a specified distance in the dark, perceiving [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]] as if it were [[Environment Rules#Dim Light|dim light]], and [[Environment Rules|dim light]] as if it were [[Environment Rules#Bright Light|bright light]]. However, the creature can't discern color in [[Environment Rules#Darkness|darkness]], only shades of gray.

### Tremorsense

A creature with tremorsense can detect and pinpoint the origin of vibrations within a specific radius, provided that the monster and the source of the vibrations are in contact with the same ground or substance such as water for aquatic creatures. Tremorsense can’t be used to detect flying or incorporeal creatures.

### Truesight

A creature with truesight can, out to a specific range, see in normal and magical darkness, see [[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects, automatically detect visual illusions and succeed on saving throws against them, and perceives the original form of a shapechanger or a creature that is transformed by magic. Furthermore, the creature can see into the Ethereal Plane.

# Food and Water

## Food

A character needs one pound of food per day and can make food last longer by subsisting on half rations. Eating half a pound of food in a day counts as half a day without food.

A character can go without food for a number of days equal to 3 + their Constitution modifier (minimum 1). At the end of each day beyond that limit, a character automatically suffers one level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]].

A normal day eating resets the count of days without food to zero.

## Water

A character needs one gallon of water per day, or two gallons of water per day if the [[Temperature and Weather Rules|weather is hot]]. A character who drinks only half that much water must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution Saving throw or suffer one level of [[Conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]] at the end of the day. A character with access to even less water automatically suffers one level of exhaustion at the end of the day.

If the character already has one or more levels of exhaustion, the character takes two levels in either case.

# Interacting with Objects

## Damaging Objects

- [[Object Rules|Objects]] are immune to poison and psychic damage
- DM determines an [[Object Rules#Statistics for Objects|objects AC and HP]]
  - and any additional immunities or resistances
- Objects always fail Strength and Dexterity \[\[saving throws, and they are immune to effects that require other saves.
- When an object drops to 0 hit points, it breaks.
- A character can also attempt a Strength check to break an object. The GM sets the DC for any such check.
