# Tokinui

## Soul Essence Quantified

These rules are a way of quantifying [[Tokinui]] to players.

# Tokinui Rules

## Gaining Tokinui

When a character finishes a short or long rest, they gain all of the Tokinui that they were awarded since they last rested.

A player character can gain Tokinui in several ways:

### Feats of Heroics

When a character performs an act of heroism, reward them Tokinui proportional to the difficulty of the feat of heroism.

- An act of heroism is a selfless act done in the name of something that the character believes in.

### Turning the Tides of Fate

When a character makes a snap decision that changes the tide of their fates, reward them Tokinui proportional to the effect of their action.

- These actions may save a character from certain death, win the respect of a rival, or otherwise alter the trajectory of a story in a significant way.

### Unearthing Ancient Secrets

When a character makes a major revelation which changes the course of their story, reward them Tokinui proportional to the obscurity of their revelation.

- Such revelations might provide insight into the machinations of a corrupt leader or even the working of the cosmos

### Achieving Major Milestones

When a character achieves a major personal milestone reward them with appropriate Tokinui

- Examples of these milestones are: defeating a rival, completing a long term goal, or overcoming a flaw

## Using Tokinui

### Manifestations

Tokinui is spent to manifest power in all sorts of distinct, repeatable ways; these methods are called [[Manifestations]]. Manifestations of Tokinui are potent and controlled direction of the energy held by Tokinui.
