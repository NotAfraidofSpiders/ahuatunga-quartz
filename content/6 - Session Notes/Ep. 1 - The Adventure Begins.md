## Summary

After nearly a month of travel together, our party of intrepid heroes finally uncovered the Accursed Cloister, buried deep in the heart of the Cardaw Wetlands. Upon their arrival, they explored an exposed cave entrance on the surface of a strange stony island.

As the party looked around the entrance, they passed through the collapsed gate and stumbled into a pile of abandoned loot infested with Ruz'set Mold. Attempting to investigate, Cygnus was blasted with a puff of spores and became infested himself. Uspia, thinking quickly, correctly  identified the mold, and recalled reading that it was killed by salt or spirits and vulnerable to acid, necrotic, and radiant damage. Villaja applied his knowledge of medicine to assist Cygnus with purging his lungs. Once Cygnus was free of his affliction, the party looted the stash and noticed that it was much darker than when they entered the dungeon.

Venturing outside the cave entrance revealed that the eclipse had begun much earlier than expected. Villaja climbed one of the vangro trees to see what was going on. As he crested the canopy, he managed to catch a glimpse of The Island's larger moon, Burit-nui, beginning to eclipse the sun, several days before the predicted eclipse of the smaller moon, Gid. Shocking as this was, Villaja slipped and fell all the way to the ground, thankfully saved from the brunt of the fall as the air gusted up to catch him.

The party briefly deliberated on what to do, eventually deciding to venture deeper into the cave with the hope that this eclipse would open the celestial gate for them. Proceeding beyond the previously explored area, the party spotted a thorny laying in wait for an ambush. Cygnus, determined to avoid repeating his earlier experience with the mold, used the spirits he found in the stash above to fashion a Molotov cocktail to incinerate the creature. A brief skirmish broke out, in which Villaja and Cygnus engaged the monstrosity in melee, while Uspia bombarded it with spells from afar. Through the course of the battle, the party learned that the moldfolk do not regenerate when subjected to cold, fire, or necrotic damage.

At the end of the now unguarded hallway, the party found a drop into a room made of masonry below. Tying off a rope and repelling down, the party found another ambush waiting for them as two moldfolk jumped from the shadows to assail our heros.

After securing the chamber, the party realized that ahead of them, carved in the likeness of a screaming man, lay the celestial gate. The wall beside the gate was carved with a pictogram of the eclipse and writing which roughly translates to, "May the primeval spirits help your soul if you proceed." Crag was able to identify that the door was made long after the rest of the dungeon was.
