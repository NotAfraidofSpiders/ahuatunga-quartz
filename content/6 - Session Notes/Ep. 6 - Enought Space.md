## Summary

After their recent insight into the purpose of the cloister, our party began deliberations on how to tackle the rest of the dungeon. The party was in agreement that they would avoid fighting Wethenor if at all possible. Once the discussion began to drift, the party continued searching the area. First, they investigated the ritual chamber adjascent to the library, before they cautiously clambored around the pile of bones to the side of the library and searched the chambers beyond. With Wade leading the charge, they found a holding chamber with the remnants of ancient restraints hanging from the walls. While beginning to leave, a skull with gems embedded in it's eye sockets caught Wade's eye.

Villaja attempted to retrieve the skull from a safe distance by calling upon the winds, which awoke the skeleton magically connected to the skull. The cursed creature thrashed desperately in the bones, as if drowning, before the party severed the skull from its spine and retrieved it. With the bejeweled skull gone, the bone pile awoke into a monstrous bone amalgamation.

The party leapt into action, with wade escaping into the library, and the rest of the party trapped in the holding chambers. The amalgamation split in two as the resulting amalgamations confronted wade and the rest of the party respectively. As his team faught valiantly to escape, Wade fled into the dungeon, cursing the amalgamation pursuing him and binding it in place with a swarm of long, thin parasitic worms. It was a quick and tense skirmish, but eventually the amalgamations were put to rest once more, finally revealing a sealed trapdoor beneath where the bone pile once lay.

The party couldn't find a way to open the trapdoor, but Crag burrowed through with the [[Ep. 4 - Discovering the Bone Zone#Loot Acquired|pickaxe he found]] in the dungeon above. The door led into a smooth stone chute, sloping into an unseen chamber below. The party quarreled as they prepared to descend, fiercely debating the order in which to approach the unknown dangers below. Eventually Crag decided to go first, followed by Villaja. Cygnus, Uspia, and Wade remained above and continued to argue for some time, until Cygnus attempted to pick Wade up and drop him down the hole.

Wade twisted away from Cygnus' grasp with startling speed, as Cygnus felt Wade's flesh squish and give way under his touch. Wade's idignant fury was instantaneous, as was his apprehension. The members of the party remaining above began their descent after declaring their intent to discuss the altercation further once they were safe to do so.

The party emerged through the mouth of a carved skull decorating the wall of the macabe chamber beneath. The walls were decorated with depictions of the dead, surrounding a large pit in the center of the chamber. The pit was easily 50' x 50' and filled to the brim with iwi bones. A polished marble pyramid rose from the center of the pit, atop which a glowing green gemstone hovered. The party cautiously threw some debris along the side of the pit, which stirred agitated ripples in the bones resting there. Wherever the ripples grew into the dead waking once more, a bright pulse from the gem returned the bones to rest.

Feeling uneasy, the party kept their investigation as brief as possible and proceeded through a doorway, down a set of stairs, and entered a chamber containing iron bars blocking the path to a gilded chest. Crag effortlessly mined around the side of the bars, leaving no further barriers between them and possible treasure. The party believed the chest was a trap, so Uspia baited Crag into opening the chest.

The party's assumptions were correct, and Crag immediately became paralyzed after opening it. As he fell backwards onto the floor, Uspia moved past him and claimed the spell scroll held within the chest.
