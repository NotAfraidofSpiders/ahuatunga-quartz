# The Curious Case of the Bone Hole

[[Ep. 2 - In Regards to the Snail Incident|<<Prev. Ep.]] | [[Ep. 4 - Discovering the Bone Zone| Next Ep. >>]]

## Summary

In the stretch of the dungeon after the debacle Villaja caused with the snail, the party split into two groups: One to loot the guard room and one to inspect the statue. The statue was defaced by the bone amalgamations, but the text carved into the base of the statue was still legible. Once translated by Uspia, it was determined to mean "Hail Wethenor: Father of Modern Magic". The guard room had quite a stash, and the party picked it clean.

Satisfied with their exploration, the party attempted to move further into the dungeon. The path was blocked by a locked door, however. Wade searched the pool in the center, and brushed up against a key. He was unable to grab it in the muck, however. Attempting to bait Cygnus into the pool, Wade feigned drowning. After a painfully long pause, Cygnus went in after him and grabbed the key. Exiting the water, Cygnus passed the key to Wade who unlocked the door.

Cygnus kept watch as the party ventured further into the cloister. Wade and Uspia searched for secrets and signs of treasure, while Uspia's familiar, Sha'ash, bartered for clams with Uspia in exchange for scouting ahead. In the end, Cygnus promised the strange bird-otter creature 18 clams, before Sha'ash was promptly freed from this plane of existence by a pit trap.

The rest of our heroes effortlessly made their way past the trap into the remains of what Uspia determined to be an old pantry. Old decaying baskets slumped on the long-rotten meat they held inside. The party deliberated briefly about how long the meat could have been in the basket, before continuing their search. Wade found a refuse pit which had been fashioned into an oubliette. Descending the ladder alone, Wade retrieved a gem inscribed with runes from the disposal pit.

Proceeding even deeper, Cygnus and Wade kept watch as Uspia searched the maze of interconnecting hallways and chambers. The party snuffed their torch to hide from an approaching snail. The party's stealth was short lived, as Wade was jumped by two bone amalgamations. The ambush alerted two bone whelks, who began to slowly pursue the party. Wade dispatched the amalgamations, and the party gave the chasing snails the slip in the tangle of corridors.

Cygnus found the key to the chamber beyond, and made to open the door. However, before he had the chance to unlock the large door forward, Wade goaded Uspia into exploring a crevasse formed by a protrusion of bone through the stone floor of a chamber. Wade secured a rope to the bone spike, which Cygnus prepared to descend. Crag unwisely made fun of Cygnus within arms reach of the monk. Cygnus rebuked him by tangling him in the rope and repelling down.

The cavern beneath was dark, but unmistakably formed around the skull of an ancient war beast. Uspia followed Cygnus and Crag and the three of them determined that the cavern around the ancient skull was the bone whelk nest. Uspia immediately tried to leave after making this discovery, but Cygnus kept her with the rest of the crew in the bone hole. As wade began to descend, Crag used his elemental affinity to cut the rope.

Thankfully, Wade managed to catch the ledge of the bone hole, and Uspia used mage hand to pass the rope back up to Wade. Quickly clambering back up, the party sealed off the bone hole to prevent any snails from following them out. Tired and nursing their wounds, the party unlocked the chamber beyond and began to fortify it to take a short rest.
