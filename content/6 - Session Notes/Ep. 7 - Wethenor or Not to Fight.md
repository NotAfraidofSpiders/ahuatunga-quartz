## Summary

After acquiring the scroll of [[Spells#Speak with Dead|Speak with Dead]], the party set up to wait out the effects of the curse on Crag while Uspia identified the spell scroll. Wade kept watch with Villaja, probing about his sanity. Cygnus studied the chamber, determining that there was little more than met the eye to uncover. Crag quickly overcame the curse upon him, and uncovered a crack in the wall behind the chest, leading to a crumbling hallway. Once the scroll was identified, the party cautiously proceeded through the crack into the passage beyond.

The end of the hall nearest the crack had collapsed into the chamber below, from which Wethenor could be heard lamenting. Despite a close call from Wade, the party evaded detection and explored down the portion of the hallway which remained in tact. A series of twisting corridores and tunnels led the party up to a large magma filled chamber, with a strange mechanism to fill and hoist a cauldron inside. A locked door covered in bones with a depression for a skull to be placed into lay to one side of the magma pool, the other led to a set of stairs back to the hall before the library.

Cautiously manuvering around the magma, the party placed the Bejeweled Skull into the slot in the door, which unlocked another pathway into the chamber containing the corpse pit. Satisified with the exploration of these chambers, the party descended towards unknown pathways once more. The party proceeded down stairs and tunnels until they found their pathway blocked by the last unidentified ghost of the cloister.

Uspia did her best to quickly communicate with the ghost, nearly immediately confirming this to be the ghost of Kaz'era, one of the two scribes of Wethenor's work. Kaz'era initially refused to let the party pass, slowly becoming more open to the idea as negotiations proceeded. Kaz'era became enraged upon being shown a copy of her work and demanded that the party do something. The agreed upon interpretation was to destroy the tome in exchange for their passage further into the dungeon.

The party was conflicted on what to do and Uspia tried as hard as she could not to destroy the book, but eventually relented and burnt it. Kaz'era was not satisfied with this outcome and grew more agitated. Something Wade said or did seemed to set Kaz'era off, and she began to escalate towards starting a fight. Crag shot first, blasting Kaz'era with a firebolt. The fight was quick and bloody. Kaz'era recklessly attacked Crag at first, but took every opportunity to enact some unknown vengance on Wade. Uspia and Cygnus hid behind Villaja, each for their own reason, as he fired arrow after arrow into Kaz'era's spectral form. Eventually, Kaz'era dissapated into a hazy mist, destroyed once and for all.

Battered but still breathing, the party finished descending the stairs. At the bottom there were two doors, one covered with runes and the other leading back towards the chamber Wethenor had occupied earlier. Investigating both doors revealed the runed set to be linked to the runed armor from the first chamber after the gate while the other door lead to a large room still occupied by a sorrowful Wethenor. The party was torn on how to proceed, with most wanting to continue exploring without confronting Wethenor. Uspia was very vocal about her desire to leave the dungeon with the knowlege and treasure gained already while the party still had their lives.

A vote was taken, where the majority elected to continue exploring without confronting Wethenor. Whether motivated by greed, curiousity, bravado, or something else entirely, Villaja stirred further discussions of whether or not the party could pragmatically challenge Wethenor by asking, "How strong is he really, though?"

Exhaustion and fear brought the tension in the party to a boil. Uspia angrily voiced her dissent for entertaining these notions, countering that the party was too fatigued to stand any chance. She put her foot down, declaring that she would not assist in fighting Wethenor, should the rest of the party decide to attempt so in vain. Wade, wary of Uspia dissapearing with treasure that rightfully belonged to the entire party, interjected and insisted that Uspia remain close enough to keep tabs on. Uspia acquiesced, though Wade mentioned that without Uspia's help any chance they did stand against Wethenor evaporated. Down a party member, an already hesitant party conceded that it would be best to leave the dungeon.

Tensions remained high as the party treked back up through the dungeon above, easily dispatching what few threats remained in their path. Upon reaching the exit of the dungeon, the party emerged back onto the surface to find the landscape transformed from how they had left it...
