# Discovering the Bone Zone

[[Ep. 3 - The Curious Case of the Bone Hole|<<Prev. Ep.]] | [[Ep. 5 - Some Lore with Wethenor| Next Ep. >>]]

## Summary

As the party found themselves in a chamber with a doorway ornamented with iwi skulls, sounds of frightened voices and a battle in the chamber beyond echoed through the ancient stone corridors from the chamber beyond. The Party descended further into the dungeon after completing a short rest. Uspia Identified the helm from the very first chamber as a modified teleportation circle.

As the party ventured onward, they found a tower on a peninsula, with a lake fed by a stream flowing from the war-beast's skull. The skull also served as the bone whelk nest. The ghosts from earlier in the dungeon were waiting above the corpses of many snails, and Wade recognized an insignia of The Atarangi's cult on the ghosts apparel.

Descending deeper the party found another statue of Wethenor, as well as a wailing ghost which seems to incite violence with its screams. A fight broke out with some bone amalgamations, which fused into a giant mass of bones before eventually being subdued by the party. A store room near the statue was looted before the merry band continued onward and Cygnus snuck the party past two bone whelks on a set of stairs using minor illusion.

Finally, the party stumbled into one of the ghosts guarding a door. Behind the door crying, gibbering, and wailing could be heard. The ghost refused to allow the party entry into the room beyond, and directed the party to proceed down another path. The party at first complied, but curiosity and doubt got the better of them, and Villaja used his affinity for the winds to open the door; at which time the crying immediately stopped, and Wethenor emerged to confront the intruders.

As Wethenor approached, Cygnus deafened himself using rags then grabbed Uspia and covered them both with a minor illusion and cast pass without trace. Wade started a crimson rite then ended it to use his Skulk disruption. Villaja climbed a wall up above the entrance to the chamber and hid. Crag dug into the wall and hid under the rubble. Uspia hid with Cygnus.
