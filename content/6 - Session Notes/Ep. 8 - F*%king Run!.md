## Summary

The party resurfaced from The Accursed Cloister to find strange vines and unnatrual pustuals growing over and an the island surrounding the mouth of the cave. Quickly assessing them, the party determined that they aren't directly dangerous, although they aren't a good thing to have around. Wade, while investigating the invasive plants, saw signs that a Matriarch of the Swamp was on the hunt nearby. After some deliberation, the party decided the safest place to wait our the matriarch's hunt was up in the canopy of the vangros.

While waiting, the party spotted lantern light flying towards the island concealing the ruin. Villaja identified the owners of the lanterns as two Gukko Riders, an older ranger and a younger greenbark. Wade noticed that both riders wore the insignia of The Brotherhood, though didn't share his observation until later. The party listened on as they conversed, unaware of the party's eavesdropping.

> [!info] Overheard Discussion
> **Croh'ombi:** ...all I'm saying is that you got assigned all the way out here in the middle of this mud-wet, soul-rot, low-spot because you never inflate War Priest Tuh'ma's ego. I got caught nabbin' a lunch that weren't mine. At least _I_ know why I can't feast with the others.
>
> **Nepet:** (_The old warrior dismounts her gukko, and strides purposfully towards her companion_) You are a fool, little Brother. The only soul a-rottin' in this mud-wet swamp is your own. _You_ are here because I specifically requested someone I don't care much for. Think before you go quick-spittin' your way into a ground-tomb.
>
> **Croh'ombi:** (_The younger soldier clumsily hops off his gukko and stumbles before slipping in the mud. The hand of his older companion shoots out and deftly grabs him by the hood of his cloak. When the soldier speaks again, it comes out slightly strangled._) Then why _are_ you here you old-dust fart?
>
> **Nepet:** (_The old warrior lets go of her grasp on her subordinate's hood, dropping him into the mud with a wet splat. She squats down, careful to keep her cloak out of the mud as she looks down, saturating her words with contempt._) _I_ am here because I have a contact in the court of a merchant king, who has verified an original text which frequently references the work done in this place. _I_ am here because I personally requested an expidition from Tuh'ma. _I_ am going to revolutionize the great works that The Brotherhood will perform for The Island in the era to come.
>
> **Croh'ombi:** (_As she monologues, the young soldier painstakingly pries free of the mud and finally comes to rest upon his knees, soaked and covered in thick black mud._)
>
> - A successful DC 14 Insight check reveals the soldiers fear of his superior
>   So, why did you request someone you weren't fond of... ma'am?
>
> **Nepet:** Because my source _also_ says that the text makes frequent note of the- how did they put it? Ah, that's right. The text makes frequent note of the, "-perilous nature of that place, especially once it is unsealed." Now get up, and go search for signs of a structure on this island. (_The warrior's cruel smile is obvious, even hidden behind her hipoki._) Ever-fast now, let's go. For tomorrow, little Brother.

As the riders began to search for the ruin, Wade noticed that the matriarch was getting ready to pounce! Acting quickly to alert the riders, he threw a pan at the great plated panther and shouted the warning, "Fucking run!" His efforts were made in vane, as the panther successfully dragged the elder ranger's gukko beneath the brackish water of the swamp. In its panic from seeing its companion dragged away, the greenbark's bird flew into the waiting vines of a carnivorous plant, stranding the riders on the island. The ranger immediately drew her bow and took a shot at where she believed Wade to be, calling out for him to reveal himself. Taking her aggression as their cue to split, the party quickly ran off, leaping from branch to vine to trunk and escaping the hostile riders.

The party made it far enough to set camp and get some much needed rest. As the party began preparing to set off once more, they realized they were being watched by an iwi with simian proportions, waiting on a branch above the party's bivouac. The iwi revealed himself to be an elemental spirit of sorts, named Wela, and believed that the party had already met him in their travels. He spoke in circles about the perils of the ruin the party was returning from and the danger yet to come. Throughout the conversation, Cygnus gifted Wela a few starlight candles. Wade traded his cloak for Wela's ancient hauiwi tunic. The conversation with Wela ended abruptly as it began when he disappeared mid sentence.
