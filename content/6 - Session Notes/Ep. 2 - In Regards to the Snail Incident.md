# In Regards to the Snail Incident

[[Ep. 1 - The Adventure Begins|<<Prev. Ep.]] | [[Ep. 3 - The Curious Case of the Bone Hole| Next Ep. >>]]

## Summary

As our party of intrepid heroes continued to descend into the depths of the Accursed Cloister, Wade observed the party cautiously, making judgements the rest could only speculate on.

The first chamber beyond the gate contained some assorted loot, and two suits of plate armor holding enchanted spears. In the first stretch of the dungeon, Uspia searched the first chamber beyond the gate as Villaja kept watch. Uspia's familiar Sha'ash investigated inside the armor to discover that they were inscribed head to toe in runes and sigils. Unsettled, but undeterred, Uspia took the helmet of one of the suits of armor before the party continued onward.

As our heroes continued on to the next stretch of the dungeon, they came across a guard chamber to the side of the main corridor. Inside, two spectral figures were assaulting a snail whose shell was made from assorted iwi bones. The party snuck past, agreeing that they wouldn't open the door or cause a scene. Proceeding forward revealed a large chamber beyond, with a hexagonal pool of water at it's center. Along the far wall of the chamber was a massive statue of a bearded man wearing a traditional Ahiwi hipoki. Unfortunately, several animated bone amalgamations made further exploration impossible without a fight.

Villaja, however, was more concerned with the well being of the snail, a bone whelk. Making a snap decision, he decided to save the whelk and lept into action to defend it from its ghostly predators. Unfortunately for his travelling companions, the noise of the melee from the corridor behind alerted the bone amalgamations to the party's presence, and a small battle immediately erupted.

The fight was quick and efficient. The bone amalgamations chased Cygnus all over the statue chamber, clambering after him with a frighteningly malicious fervor. Terrified by the discovery of undead monsters and their violent nature, Wade hid in the water at the center of the chamber while his companions dealt with the threat at hand. Back in the hallway and guard room, Uspia helped to save Villaja's life from the ghosts he attacked. Cygnus and Villaja took quite a beating, though Uspia and Wade got out unscathed.

As the dust settled and everyone checked their wounds, Wade turned to Villaja and said, "What the fuck happened to not opening that door?"
