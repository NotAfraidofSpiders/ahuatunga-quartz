# The Ahuatungan Calendar

The system of chronicling time on the island.

## A Quick Overview:

The Ahuatungan calendar is broken up into six seasons, each of which has two months. This makes for a total of 12 months in a year. Each month consists of three twelve-day weeks, for a total of 36 days a month. Each season is seventy-two days and each year is 432 days.

# Weeks

The Ahuatungan calendar uses twelve-day weeks. Each day of the week contains the suffix "-henga," the [[Ahuatungan Language|Ahuatungan]] word for day. The week starts on Mohenga (Mo) and ends on Ahuahenga (Ahu), the sacred day.
