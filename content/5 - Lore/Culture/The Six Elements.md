It is well established that there are six primordial elements, from which all things worldly can be formed: Fire, air, water, ice, lightning, and earth. The elements are the very essence of the universe, and a gift from Ahuatunga, the god of creation. The elements themselves can be recombined in infinite ways. According to legend, the earliest shamans, holy-women, and alchemists first learned to harness elements they were not attuned to. In doing so they learned the first arcane magic.

# Primeval Spirits

The Primeval Spirits are the embodiment of the elements. While it is true that elementals take many shapes and forms as the elements will, the primeval spirits act as the will of the elements themselves.

# Elemental Affinity

Iwi have always had a close connection with the elements as one of Ahuatunga's final creations.  For as long as history has existed, each iwi has had an affinity for one of the six elements.
