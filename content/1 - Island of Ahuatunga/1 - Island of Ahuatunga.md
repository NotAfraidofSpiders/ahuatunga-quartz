# The Island of Ahuatunga

The island of Ahuatunga is a mystical and vibrant island of myth. Legends exist which declare that it is the ancestral homeland of all Iwi. The island is comprised of six distinct regions, each of which is the domain of one of the six elements. The [[Perigrine Iwi]] lived upon in ancient times, but for the past millennia its lands have belonged to the penitent survivors of the Shadow War.

## The Six Regions

Ahuatunga is comprised of six unique elemental regions, one for each of [[the six elements]]. Each elemental region has many geographic and ecological features, not all of which have been charted in the thousand years since iwi returned to the island.

### Ihu - First Snowhome

### Kauae - Elemental Homeland of Air

### Mangai - The Blaze Eternal

### Motara - Cradle of Lightning

### Naho - Domain of Water

### Tiro - Earthen Foundation
